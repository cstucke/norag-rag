**Infobox: Cave 4 - Baby Cave**
**Description**
- **Official Names:** Baby Cave
- **Player Given Names:** Cave 4
- **Added in:** v0.01

**Cave 4** has an entrance to the south of the Lakeside Village (northeast of the yacht). It contains one Virginia and several Mutant babies (hence the name; multiple piles of dead Mutant babies can also be found).  
It's part of Cave 2 - Hanging Cave, but listed separately in the survival guide's to-do list as "*keep exploring the hanging cave*".
