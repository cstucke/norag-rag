**Teeth** are material items that were added in update v0.01 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Teeth** are used with sap to incrementally and permanently upgrade the damage bonus of a melee weapon with each **tooth** added, though it will slightly reduce the speed. They will drop when a mutant is hit with any weapon or killed. The most efficient way to harvest **teeth** is by hitting the mutants repeatedly with low damage melee weapons or items, such as the upgraded stick, animal heads, meat etc. Teeth drop chance is about 10 to 15%. Damage has no effect on teeth drop chance.

All Creepy Mutants, including Armsies, Virginias, Cowmen, and Mutant Babies do not drop **teeth**.
