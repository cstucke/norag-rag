## Gameplay

**Nitrogen Tanks** can be activated by damaging them with a Weapon. After 4 seconds of "hissing" the tank explodes dealing 25 damage. There are 4 variations of tank placements, mostly placed in pairs. If two tanks are being activated at the same time, the explosion will deal the same damage as with one tank. There are 8 tanks in the Boss room that can be used to defeat End Boss.

## Locations

**Nitrogen Tanks** can only be found in Sahara Laboratory. There are 23 tanks in 13 locations.
