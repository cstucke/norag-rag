The **Small Table** is a furniture building that was added in update v0.63 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

* It can be known as the side table or small table.
* Can be used to place Arrow Basket and Bone Basket on it.
* Great decoration for your living room in your house.
* It is quite a bit smaller than the Table
