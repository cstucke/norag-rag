**Rabbit furs** (also known as **rabbit pelts** or **rabbit skins**) are craftable items that can be collected from dead rabbits, it was added in update v0.12 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

They are taken off before rabbit meat is collected. The player can hold 10 in their inventory and can store 15 **rabbit furs** on a skin rack. Unlike lizard skins, **rabbit skins** can't be worn or stored on an armor rack. If a rabbit is killed by a fire weapon, such as a flare gun, fire arrow or molotov, this will burn the **rabbit skin**, which removes the **rabbit skin** completely. However, this will cook the rabbit which allows for immediate consumption of the rabbit meat.
