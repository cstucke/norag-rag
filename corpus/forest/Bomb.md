The **bomb**, known as *explosives* are an explosive weapon that were added in update v0.01 to *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

Thrown bombs will take 4 seconds to explode and slide on the ground, both which make it hard to hit faster targets. The sliding can be avoided by combining them with sap to craft sticky bombs.

Most of the time when animals, cannibals and mutants are killed by the explosion will be blown to bits, leaving no items behind.

Bombs can also be used to destroy:

* Walls inside the caves to open up passages.
* secret caches hidden over the whole peninsula surface to reveal old gun parts.
* trees to gather logs. (explosives, except for head bombs, can also be placed directly on trees)
* flora, cannibal effigies, laptops, crates, cardboard boxes and other small objects.
* buildings.

They can also be used to craft the stronger, but harder to use head bombs or set up as explosive wire traps.  
Because bombs can easily get rid of unwanted **AND** wanted structures, they should be used with caution.
