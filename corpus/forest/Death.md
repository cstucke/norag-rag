# Death
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Death
Categories: Gameplay
Revision: 74644

**Death** is a game mechanic that occurs when a player's health reaches zero. This behavior is different between Singleplayer and Multiplayer.

## Death in Singleplayer

The first time the player loses all their health, they will fall to the ground and pass out. Then a short cut-scene will play where two cannibals are dragging the player away, which after momentarily, the player loses consciousness. Afterwards, the player reawakens in total darkness and will be instructed to take out the Lighter. Once the Lighter has been lit, the player will notice they are hanging upside down from the top of a cave. Then facing downwards will reveal the Plane Axe, which the player can pick up and use, to cut the binds they were hanging from to become free.

At this point, the player can roam the cave or escape. Escape is highly advised, as the player's health is critically low, and they could get knocked again while exploring the cave if Medicine was not already in the inventory (see maps).

The second time the player gets knocked, the game-over screen will pop up showing a red background and the dead player alongside a short dramatic song and the total number of days they have survived. Surviving two in-game days will regain the player the ability to get dragged once again by the cannibals. Effectively acting as an extra life to prevent the game-over screen from showing up.

After a game-over, users can choose to load the last saved game or start over.

## Death in Multiplayer

Any time a player's health reaches zero, instead of dying or being taken to a cave by cannibals, players will be knocked out, causing them to drop everything and fall to their knees.

The screen will be grayed out with blood splatters surrounding it along with a white skull in the middle while the player gasps and breathes heavily. Other players on the game will see an icon above a downed player's location and will have a limited amount of time to revive them before they die. Holding the interaction key on a downed player will revive them if in close range. If a downed player gets hit by another player, they will die.

During the process of death, incapacitated players cannot do anything except for pressing the interaction key to restart, sending them back to the plane crash site and losing all items from their inventory. Items can be picked up again after downed players have respawned. However, any weapon upgrades will be gone. If the host quits the game, all dropped items will be lost. In addition of losing all their items, players that get respawned will also have their stats reset to their starting values.

Players who aren't hosting may rejoin and load a save to avoid this loss.

### Death can occur from the following:

* Creepy Mutants
* Cannibals
* Fall damage
* Damage from falling trees
* Glitches
* Sharks, Boars, and Crocodiles
* Poison
* Consuming spoiled meat
* Drinking polluted water
* Fire
* Drowning
* Dehydration
* Starvation
* Freezing to death
* Explosions

## Known Bugs

* After you drown, you can hear the player breathing heavily.
* If you die while you're on a rope, (by burning or with explosives), you can spin during the death animation and even spin the cannibals in the death screen.
* (Multiplayer) If in Final zone after elevator ride, and you decide to have a "fun" duel, whoever dies, Respawns at the plane...
