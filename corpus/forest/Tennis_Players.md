The **Tennis Players** were a small group of Passengers on Coupon Air Flight 111.
All of the players were killed by the Mutants and their bodies can be found in game.

## known Players

* A. Preston
* T. Mecca
* A. Landsberg
* F. Hamilton
* C. Boris
* M. Galbraith
* M. Best
* T. Bell
