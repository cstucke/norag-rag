The **Endnight Magazine** is a decorative item that cannot be collected or interacted with, it was added in update ? in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **Endnight Magazine** can be found in many places across the game. It can be found on the plane and caves. When you build a Log Cabin, a few copies will appear on the floor. Endnight is the developer of *The Forest*. The image is from their first game, which they stopped developing to focus on The Forest.
