The **Shipwreck Cave** or Cave 11 is found in the very southwest corner of the map slightly further from the anchor symbol and west of the yacht. It can be entered by swimming into the wreck of an old ship (giving it its name) and swimming down into a cave entrance found in a hole of the ship's hull. The cave itself is very small and has no enemy mutants that spawn naturally. In the center of the wall on the far end of the cave, there is a drawing of what seems to be the ship before it was destroyed. It is recommended to carry a rebreather when exploring the cave.
