**Leaf Pile Trap** is a trap made out of 50 leaves and can be placed and lit to make a wall of fire, connecting multiple ones will expand the trap for a longer wall.

## Gameplay

The trap will last for 20s and can be lit with molotov, molotov wire trap, fire arrow and lighter.

Combining it with a molotov trip wire trap will light the **leaf pile trap** on fire when the molotov wire trap is triggered.

**Leaf pile traps** can also be used to ignite basic fires, fire pits, standing fires and bonfires if the **leaf pile trap** is built close enough to it.

When lit, these traps will ignite any other nearby leaf pile traps (the distance is minimal). They can be placed under many walls, fences and structures. A line of leaf pile traps under defensive walls can be ignited to help defend them when they are under attack. Even a single trap underneath Defensive wall gates / entrances to your base can be helpful.

Though often overlooked these traps are extremely cheap, leaves being easy to come by, and very strong. They will set the player on fire if he stands to close when lighting it. Leaf pile traps are single use.
