The **skull lamp** is a decorative building and light source that was added in update v0.15 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

A **skull lamp** is useful for improving the player's ability to see at night since they (unlike fires) do not need to be re-lit. They can be placed for lit pathways around the player's base. The large number of skulls in caves may indicate encouragement from the developers to place **skull lamps** in caves. Placing lots of these in a cave may affect performance of your game, though this depends of whether the game loads the cave system and above ground system at the same time.
