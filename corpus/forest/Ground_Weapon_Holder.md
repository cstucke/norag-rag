The **ground weapon holder** is a weapon storage building that was added in v0.67. It is a variant of the Wall Weapon Rack which is where it is found in the book. It is placed on the ground or floor type buildings, just like the weapon rack

## Gameplay

To build a **ground weapon holder**, the player must select the Wall Weapon Rack in the book. Then aim the cursor at the ground and it will transform into the **ground weapon holder**.
The **ground weapon holder** allows players to store most Weapons outside of their inventory. It's also a good way to share weapons with other players. To place an item on the rack you need to equip it, walk up to the rack and press 'C'. To take a weapon from the rack you press 'E' on whatever weapon you want to take.

It is placed on the ground and looks similar to a chopping block. From the images below, certain weapons look better than others. Axes and machetes look the best, others just sit on top.

## Storage

The **ground weapon holder** can store one of the following weapons/tools:  
 Chainsaw  
 Climbing Axe  
 Club  
 Crafted Axe  
 Crafted Bow  
 Crafted Club  
 Flare Gun  
 Flintlock Pistol  
 Incendiary Spear  
 Katana  
 Machete  
 Modern Axe  
 Modern Bow  
 Rock  
 Rusty Axe  
 Slingshot  
 Tennis Racket  
 Upgraded Rock  
 Upgraded Spear  
 Upgraded Stick  
 Weak Spear  
  
It **CANNOT** store the following weapons/tools:  
 Plane Axe  
 Repair Tool  
 Turtle Shell
