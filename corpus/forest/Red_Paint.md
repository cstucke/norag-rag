The **Red Paint** is a special item that can be worn by the player, similar to mud. It was added as a decoration in update v0.45, though made a use-able in update v0.49 in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Currently, **red paint** is contained in open paint buckets, and can be found in several locations on the Peninsula. It is often accompanied by a paintbrush. Once picked up, the player will automatically wear it.
The cannibals, upon witnessing the player wearing the **red paint**, can act in a passive manner (though they can still become hostile upon attack). Their interactions with the player consists of: following and staring at him; stepping back if the player tries to approach them; some will even kneel and pray at him as if was some sort of sacred being, or other divine entity that has significant value to the cannibal people. **Red paint** can have an effect on all cannibals in the area, causing them all to kneel. However, if the player starts attacking, the cannibals will become hostile again. Additionally, kneeling cannibals will rise up after a short while if not killed, and either become hostile again or go back to kneeling. Pales will just stay kneeling sometimes. Creepy Mutants are unaffected.

While **red paint** will only spawn in 8 locations, and can only be applied once per game session, the buckets will replenish whenever the player quits and reloads the game.

*Note: The **red paint** will be removed with rain or if the player starts to swim in any water source.*

## Locations

**Red Paint** can be found:

* in one of the cannibal longhouses overlooking the ocean.
* in one of the River longhouses with slides into the water.
* near the Main Village.
* against the cliff wall in the Fertile Lands.
* in Cave 7 - Chasm Cave, as the player enters the climbing entrance and continues to the waterfall room, the paint will be found on a ledge on the way down to the bottom of the cave.
* in Cave 7 - Chasm Cave by the **red man cave painting**
* within the Cave 2 - Hanging Cave at the same room as the modern axe.
* Multiple can be found in the Sahara Laboratory.

## Background

It is theorized that the Sahara company, in their research lab, used fear-conditioning with their patients, who later became the cannibals. This can be evidenced by a number of things.

1. The entry hallway to Sahara is bright red all the way through, with red walls and floors.
2. The secure doors that the player cannot enter, throughout the facility, are bright red.
3. The red paint induces a passive, non-hostile state in the cannibals, who will sit and kneel, or at the very least, back away.
4. Dr. Cross knows to use Red Paint to induce the passive state in the cannibals.
5. Red paint can be located all over the island, notably in three different large cannibal camps.

Because of all of this, it can be deduced that Sahara fear-conditioned red into the cannibals, making them associate the color with pain or death or something else unpleasant, or perhaps something pleasant such as rewards for kneeling instantly. This was done to better control the cannibals, as they grew into larger and larger numbers due to the increasing extensiveness of Sahara's tests. Dr. Cross uses the red paint because he used to work at the lab and knew this. The buckets around the peninsula were put there either by him for easy access at all times, or perhaps by Sahara employees a while ago so they could safely traverse the peninsula.

Alternatively, the cannibals found around the island were most likely aware of the artifact and its powers before Sahara Labs purchased the island. Given that Dr. Cross was able to harness the power of the artifact and create mutants/other life forms the cannibals respect Dr. Cross and the color red because they are aware to some extent of his capabilities. This would explain why they kneel before him, they see him as a God capable of wielding the power of the artifact and as someone that runs the Lab that now surrounds the artifact. The mutants do not have this association to Dr. Cross or the artifact and attack the player regardless if he is covered in Red Paint or not, because they do not carry the religious association that the island natives had.

It is also possible that the numerous cans of Red Paint are from renovations that were happening in the Sahara Lab before it was abandoned, since multiple cans can be found in the Maintenance area.
