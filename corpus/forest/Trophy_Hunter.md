# Trophy Hunter
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Trophy_Hunter
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74495

## Description

*Kill all animal types and display heads*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

See Head Trophy or Animal Heads.

Most animals work in single player or multiplayer mode. However, geese and their heads are very buggy and may require extra steps. This achievement is possible to obtain in Creative mode.

### Requirements

Crocodile head  
 Deer head  
 Goose head  
 Lizard head  
 Boar head  
 Raccoon head  
 Rabbit head  
 Seagull head  
 Shark head  
 Squirrel head  
 Tortoise head

## Console Commands method

The Trophy Hunter achievement can be quite frustrating to obtain due to Geese being frustrating to kill while retaining the head. You can use console commands to obtain the heads. Type in `developermodeon` in the main menu, then start your game. Once in game, press `F1` then type any of the following commands, depending on your needs.

|  | Animal Head | Add item | Spawn item |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Rabbit Head | `additem 185` | `spawnitem 185` |
|  | Boar Head | `additem 186` | `spawnitem 186` |

## Tips

### Geese

* Geese "sink" when killed. It may be necessary to build a platform under the surface of the water to kill them and collect their head efficiently
* Geese heads disappear almost immediately after death. The player must spam the `E` key while killing the goose to collect the head
* Try scaring the geese to the edge of the lake (by swimming near them to direct them) then kill them while you are on the edge of the land - then quickly spamming the `E` key is best
* Mounting the head in multiplayer sometimes does not work for geese, and may need to be done in single player mode.
* Good way to get its head is to build a raft in the lake and move it in the direction where the geese will fly when they will fly away. Build also a camp so that you can save and sleep (if needed). Have your bow ready and try to shoot one of the goose when they fly away and they "approach" your raft. If done correctly, dead goose will fall onto your raft and just go for the head.

### Shark Head

Shark Heads can only be obtained from dead sharks on the beaches. Simply attack the neck of a dead shark with a bladed weapon. The head will come off within about 2 to 4 hits. Keep in mind that dead sharks despawn once you remove the head, ensure you don't waste all the heads.

### Mutant babies

You can also kill a mutant baby and collect its head. If you put its head as a trophy, it will count towards the achievement (i.e. it will be sufficient to display only 12 animal heads).
