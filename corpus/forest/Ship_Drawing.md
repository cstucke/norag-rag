Located right above the crossbow. Depicts the ship when it was intact hundreds of years ago. Appears to be a large Chinese Junk ship, suggesting that the ship was bought from China and used by the Missionaries to travel to the Peninsula. The anchors on the southern coast of the Peninsula most likely came from this ship. How the ship crashed is unknown, but the Crossbow may have had something to do with it (based on the bolts found shot in the side of the stern).
