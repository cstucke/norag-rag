The **Yacht magazine** is a story item found on board the Yacht. It uses the same examine animation as toy pieces and other similar items. It can be found near the milk carton, the Virginia photo, and some obelisk drawings. Upon discovery, it is added to the Notes section of the Survival Guide. It is dated to 1984, 2 years later than the June '82 Yacht Photo. At the same time, the Charles survivor script is dated to 1984 as well, suggesting a link between the yachters and the film camp.
