# Bestiary
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Bestiary
Categories: Survival Guide
Revision: 58262

**Bestiary** was a section of the survival guide that contained 4 blank pages when the player first opens the book. As the player encounters more cannibals, the book gets filled with drawings as well as the names of said cannibals and their traits/weaknesses. In order to add an enemy, the player must be hit by it first. It was removed in v1.0.
