## The Basics

**The Sahara Laboratory**, also known as the **Research Laboratory** or simply **Lab** [Property of Sahara Therapeutics] is an area located deep under the Peninsula. To access the **Lab**, the player must go through the Vault Door, located at the end of Cave HC at the bottom of the sinkhole. The majority of the story's plot is discovered here. Upon leaving, the lab cannot be visited again during the same playthrough, as the entrance is blocked by a cave-in.

## History

The **Sahara Laboratory** was founded on some unknown date (most likely 1999 or 2000 based on the technology seen) on the Peninsula that constitutes the playable area of The Forest. It runs deep underground, with levels B-G all being below the cave systems, though it also reaches high into the mountains with an observatory. The main area of the lab that’s explored during *The Forest* is Level G, at the very bottom. The **lab** is owned by Sahara Therapeutics, who purchased the peninsula in order to conduct its genetic experiments. Sahara most likely gave the Peninsula the name “Site 1” (based on the canon ending cutscene).

Mathew Cross worked here for some time before the events of The Forest. He was fired due to using the company equipment found in the lab for personal reasons. He most likely caused the breakout after being fired, resulting in the state it’s in now.

The lab seemingly dealt with genetics mostly, though it also had a supernatural component with the obelisks.

The observation rooms were set up to monitor and record patients and their genomes, particularly noting transformations such as the Armsy or Virginia mutations. It appears that **Sahara** induced these mutations in patients as a way to explore increasing longevity (possibly related to the obelisks).

At some point, something went terribly wrong in the **lab**, with a possible cannibal/mutant uprising that left the facility overrun. Dead bodies that appear to be cannibals and/or research patients are strewn across the lab, most often near the observation rooms. Many parts of the facility are covered in broken glass, bloody body parts, and upturned furniture, in addition to parts of the ceiling and piping having collapsed. Cannibals, babies, and some mutants still linger in the facility. It appears that the facility was left in a hurry, as evidenced by the multiple meals left out in the cafeteria (though these could belong to Dr. Cross, Megan, or Timmy). Additionally, it seems that the evacuation (if there was one) was not too far prior to the events of The Forest, as there is a scientist, who seemingly committed suicide by hanging himself, located deeper in the lab, with minimal signs of decay, though he could have stuck around trying to survive for a while after perhaps being left behind in the evacuation. Further signs of a struggle come from the man-made barricades piled against doors, notably at the door leading to the room with the hanged scientist.

The **laboratory**, connected through a series of ventilation shafts, piping, and natural cave formations, has a few main areas:

All of the enemies encountered in the lab are "off". Each seems weaker than normal, particularly the Armsy and the Cannibals. All enemies are colored differently from normal.

The lab contains:

* 7 mutant babies
* 1 Armsy
* 3 Skinny Mutants
* 1 End Boss, the Obelisk Victim/Megan

## Navigating through

### Vault Door

**Vault Door** is the only available entrance to the **lab** that leads to the **Entrance Hallway**. Keycard is required to open the **Vault Door**.

* Items

Timmy Drawing very close to the door

### Entrance Hallway

Also known as "red room" or "entry hall". First room after the Vault Door painted in red color possibly to scare off any enemy that could somehow break through the Vault door. Timmy has made his last drawing from this room. There is a climbing rope leading to the ’catwalk’/maintenance shaft above the hallway.

* Items

Red Paint at the end of the hallway

### Entrance Catwalk

This catwalk can only be accessed using a climbing rope in **Entrance Hallway**. It goes above the entire red entrance hallway and another red hallway that is not accessible by the player. It leads to **Access Corridor 7 - Level G** and cannot be accessed again once dropping down to the access corridor.

* Items

2 Meds  
 5 Bones

### Access Corridor 7 - Level G

Maintenance corridor is accessed by dropping down from the catwalk and has stairs that lead to the utility room. Inaccessible door behind where the player drops down leads to the inaccessible corridor seen earlier. The sign on the wall imply other access corridors exist on the other levels.

* Items

1 Nitrogen Tank

### Utility Room

Room containing a few toolboxes and ventilation parts and it leads to the "Crawl Space/Ventilation Shaft" and cannot be accessed again after dropping down to the shaft. The room was later used by Dr. Mathew Cross who made a "Megan shrine" by using crosses and pasting a couple of Megan Emails on the wall.

* Items

2 Nitrogen Tanks  
 Email - Megan  
 Red Paint

### Crawl Space

This maintenance crawl space or ventilation shaft can be accessed by dropping down from the **Utility room** and cannot be exited the same way. It contains some piping, lighting and tools. A part of it was used by an unknown child as a secret hideout. The end of the shaft has climbing rope that leads to the **Cafeteria**.

* Items

2 Meds  
 4 Cloth

### Cafeteria

The cafeteria is accessed using a climbing rope from the ventilation shaft under it. It contains tables, vending and coffee machines and other consumables. Walls and tables are packed with scientific pages, books and experiments. It is a great place for players to stock up with supplies. Broken ventilation shaft with pipes at the far end of the cafeteria can be used to progress further. An "active" version of the Cafeteria can be seen in Camcorder tape **Lunch with Megan**.

### Ventilation Shaft

Ventilation shaft that can be accessed from its broken part above the **Cafeteria**, above the inaccessible hallway and leads to the **Children's Ward**. It is very dark and only has a drawing of unknown origin.

### Children's Ward

This room contains hospital beds, children's books and toys, scientific papers and magazines, and medical equipment. The room is accessible from the **Ventilation shaft** and cannot be exited from the same way. Children's ward leads to the hallways with **Observation rooms**. An inaccessible door near the **Ventilation Shaft** leads to a hallway seen from the **Cafeteria**.

* Items

3 Cloth  
 Magazine - Ethical Scientist

### Observation Rooms

Observation rooms are accessible from **Children's Ward** and lead to the broken maintenance shaft. Observation rooms consists of 2 Main hallways, 2 access rooms, 2 back-hallways labeled as 258 (1 accessible) and rooms labeled "Observation Room A-H". Access rooms and Main hallways are equipped with sprinkler system that can be activated with fire. Rooms labeled as "Observation Room A-H" are children's rooms containing children's toys, books, beds and other furniture that were used to observe the process of mutations as seen in Camcorder tape **Side Effects**. Most of the rooms were violently destroyed in attacks similar to the one seen in Camcorder tape **Armsy Breakout**. The various windows scattered around this area are possible replacements for the windows broken in **Armsy Breakout**.

* Enemies

Mutant babies  
 1 Armsy

* Items

3 Cloth  
 Megan's Crayons

* Achievements

Make it Rain - *Set off the sprinklers in end game*

### Resurrection Obelisk Room

Big room with Resurrection Obelisk and a bed in the middle. Likely the resting place of the Obelisk, with the room (and this floor of the lab) being built around it. Evidence of this can be found in a photo of the Obelisk next to a missionary camp, presumably taken before the lab’s construction. Accessed from **Incubation rooms** and leads to the lounge access hallway and the hallway requiring Keycard Two. The Resurrection Obelisk can be seen in Camcorder tape labeled **Opening the artifact**. This room is also connected to the smaller incubation room on the right side and a small office on the left side. The room was last used by Dr. Mathew Cross, who tried to resurrect his daughter Megan Cross. He can be found dead in the small office, stabbed to death with crayons by Megan, who left bloodied footsteps leading all the way to the **Atrium/Boss Room**.

### Atrium

Atrium (also known as the Boss room) is by far the biggest room of the **Laboratory**. From the design of the room, it is possible that the Atrium was built as the main entrance lobby for the facility, judging by the massive ‘skylight’ and the doors (one of which is blocked). It is the room where the player finds Megan Cross who mutates into End Boss. It is possible that Megan tried to escape through here. The floor of the room is also see through, letting the player look down into the caves below.

### Business Offices

Offices and hallways that are filled with gore and remains of the incident that happened. Mathew appears to have made a shrine in one of the meeting rooms. He may have also been responsible for some of the killing, as a severed head with a drill stuck in it can be found in the same room, alongside a severed head stuck in a water jug. Accessed from Cave Science - Sahara Cave and goes in the (partially collapsed) tight passage and hallway that leads to the **Elevator**.

### Observatory/Observation Level

Observatory can be only accessed with the **Elevator** and cannot be exited the same way. The main control table contains the coordinates of the Second Plane, which can be shot down by activating the Power Obelisk. The Observatory was briefly used by Sahara (led by Mathew based on the Camcorder tape **Second Artifact Test**) to study the Power Obelisk and the effects of its EMP on the residents of The Peninsula. There is another **elevator** that leads to the Artifact Cave.
