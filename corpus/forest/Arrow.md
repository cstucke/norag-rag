**Arrows** are craftable projectile weapons added in update v0.05 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Arrows come in five types: normal, modern, bone, poison, and fire. They are used in conjunction with the **bow** as a **ranged weapon**, and are therefore useless without a bow. After an enemy is dead, the player can collect the **arrows** used from the body, though **fire** and **poison arrows** will become **normal arrows** again. Additionally, if the player shoots and misses, it is possible to collect the **arrow** from the ground where it landed.

The bow and **arrows** are most useful for ranged combat, being generally much too slow in close quarters. The player can stand on a platform or cliff ledge and pick off the **enemies** below. **Normal arrows** are suggested for animals, as most require only one hit to kill. **Fire, Poison**, and **Bone arrows** are intended for cannibals and mutants (with the exception of babies, who should also be hit with **normal arrows**, as they only require one hit to kill).

On the PlayStation 4 version, the player must look down and press `R1` to change between **arrow** types.
