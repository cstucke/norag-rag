## Lukas Patrus

* End Credits role: 3D ART

**Lukas Patrus on Artstation**
I've worked on Endnight's The Forest for about 3 years now.  
With it now finally released I decided to upload some of my work that has accrued over the years.  
These are just a random selection of the 200+ assets that I worked on for the game.  
Looking through some of my older work I can appreciate how I progressed as an artist.

### Enemies

* gallery link

Depending on how complex the enemy design was, it took around 4-7 days to create.  
Turnover was pretty quick as we updated the game pretty frequently.  
I really appreciated the freedom the game director gave me to work in my own style.  
After a quick blockout in Zbrush most meshes got detailed in Zbrush and were prepped for baking in Marmoset and texturing in Substance Painter.

### Player Variations/Clothing

* gallery link

Most of the clothing took 2-3 days. It was first created in Marvelous, send to Zbrush, retopo in Maya, then baked in Marmoset and textured in Substance Painter.  
Really loved how much the player loved the different styles of clothing.  
The proportions were a bit off in the rig that the game was using so I had to bend towards that for technical reasons, so they're a bit off in the renders.

### Armor

* gallery link

Most of the different kinds of armor took 4-7 days with most of them being on the shorter side, I decided to show these 2 as the other kinds only really make sense out of FPS-Perspective.  
Before investing a ton of time in Zbrush or Marvelous creating the models I first started with a rough sketch to be send off to the game director.  
With his feedback in mind I would refine the idea in 3D before commiting on starting the highpoly model.
