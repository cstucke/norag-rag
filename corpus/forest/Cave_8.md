**Infobox: Cave 8 - Sinkhole Cave**
**Description**
- **Official Names:** Sinkhole Cave
- **Player Given Names:** Cave 8
- **Added in:** v0.01

**Cave 8** is a part of Cave 7, but is still its own dedicated cave system. It's officially called the 'Sinkhole Cave' although due to **Cave 7** & **8** being mostly merged, and considered as one under the name 'Cave 7', **Cave 7'**s sinkhole exit (actually part of **Cave 8**) is often called by the same name.

Unlike Cave 3, Endnight Games did not make **Cave 8** more independent from Cave 7; Cave 3 was originally a part of Cave 1 in older versions before it was made its own distinct cave.
