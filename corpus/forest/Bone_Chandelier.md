The **Bone Chandelier** is a ceiling fitting light source building that was added in update v0.63 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

It operates as a decoration only, it does provide a fair amount of light though it doesn't provide any warmth. It does hang low, which means the player can collide with it. It originally had no clipping though clipping was added in update v0.72. It is recommended to build it high enough to avoid colliding with it. It is a very expensive light source.
