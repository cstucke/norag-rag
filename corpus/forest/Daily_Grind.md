## Description

*Drink from coffee machine*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

You must drink from the coffee machine in the Sahara Laboratory. To access the **Lab**, the player must go through the Vault Door, located at the end of Cave HC at the bottom of the sinkhole.

### Requirements

drinking **Coffee** from the coffee machine  
 Rebreather to access the location  
 Climbing Axe to access the location  
 Keycard to access the location
