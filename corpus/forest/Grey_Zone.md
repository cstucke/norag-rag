# Grey Zone
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Grey_Zone
Categories: Stats
Revision: 72284

The **Grey Zone** is when a players health is reduced to under 10 health points. Certain unique effect take place when a player is in the **grey zone**

## Gameplay

The **Grey Zone** will cause the players screen to turn grey in color, hence the name. This is primarily to indicate that the player is near death, though it also activates a few mechanics that can help the player survive:

* Adrenaline Rush - This has a cool down of 2 minutes, it will causes the player to gain back 50% of their stamina over 1 second.
* Increased Weapon Damage - The players damage will increase by 30%
* Last Chance - When an enemy hit that should cause death while player health is above grey zone instead brings player to 1 HP. The player must be above the grey zone for this to occur
* Health generation - The player will begin to regenerate health up to 10. This will take the player out of the **grey zone**.
* Block reduction - Blocking is reduced whilst in the **grey zone**. Whilst this isn't a large amount, blocking with the Katana or Machete in the **grey zone** will kill the player.
