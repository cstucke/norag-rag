The **Center Cannibal Village** is a cannibal built location.

## Gameplay

The **center cannibal village** is almost in the direct center of the map, it is slightly south from it. This is a hub for cannibals, they will often pass through here. This is one of the more dangerous **villages** in the game due to the amount of patrol routes that funnel through here. Surprisingly, the sacred tree that is a short distance from it is a very safe place to build, it isn't on a **patrol route**.
