Vending Machines can be found at the end of the game. They are used to purchase soda or snack.

**SPOILER** :Vending machines can be found in Sahara Laboratory Cafeteria and in some rooms.

Soda and Snacks can be purchased with 1 Cash.

## Water-Vending Machine

Water-vending machines can be also found in the Lab.

You'll get clear water to drink.

The drinking method is similar to drinking from lakes.

You can get the Big Spender achievement by purchasing a Soda or Candy from the vending machine(s)
