The **bone basket** is a storage building that was added in update v0.30 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **bone basket** allows the storage of up to 86 bones. It is good in multiplayer so that every one can have a place to easily share bones. It can be placed on tables and side tables. It is very small and very weak.
