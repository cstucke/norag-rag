# Flies
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Flies
Categories: Animal, Creature
Revision: 69424

**Infobox: Fly**
**Details**
- **Location(s):** Near corpses left alone for a while

**Flies** are merely decorative creatures that spawn around most dead entities on the peninsula.
They generally spawn after a short amount of time has passed around carcasses of wildlife or the corpses of cannibals and mutants. This is a good indication that the player has left the meat long enough to rot.

**Flies** can also be found around existing decorative corpses; typically blackened; across the peninsula, for instance in cannibal villages or at abandoned camps.
They can often be heard before the player notices the body, especially in the caves.

**Flies** cannot be interacted with and serve no purpose other than to provide deeper immersion. They are not listed in the Nature Guide of the Survival Guide.
