The **Birdhouse** is a structure that was added in v0.63. Once built will attract birds, who will likely drop feathers.

## Gameplay

* Can be built on many types of structures, not just trees.
* The bird house can only gather a maximum of 10 feathers.
* If bird are attacked while on the structure, it may cause damage or destroy the **birdhouse**. Using a repairing tool will prevent that, but still kills the birds.
* In multiplayer, once the host collects the feathers the birdhouse's storage is reset. Players should gather from the birdhouse before the host does to increase yield. (Possibly a bug)
