**The Dark Haired Man book** is a story item that was added in update v0.32 of ***The Forest***. The book is written by Sam Sounde, who also wrote the survival guide with the games protagonist, Eric LeBlanc.

## Gameplay

**The Dark Haired Man book** can be found in Cave 6 - Lawyer Cave. It is near the lawyers heads and the fortune pickups.

* Picking up one of the books will erase all duplicate Dark Haired Man Books from their location/s.
* Using `giveallstoryitems` command will delete this book from all locations in the world.
* Does not appear in notes or inventory after pickup.
