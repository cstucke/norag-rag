The **Virginia Photo,** or **the photo of Virginia,** is a story item added in v0.18 to *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

It is simply a photograph of a Virginia, one of the peninsula's many mutants. Unlike most items, it doesn't appear in the player's inventory once picked up, but is instead added to the Notes section of the Survival Guide.
It can be found in the sailboat along with the Yacht Magazine and several drawings of the resurrection obelisk.

## Theory

It can be assumed that the yacht owners knew somewhat what they were getting into before coming to the island, based on the obelisk drawings. The **Virginia Photo** would be taken while the group were exploring the caves, most likely Cave 4 based on the corpse that could be found nearby.
