**Tree Sap** is a collectible material item which was added in v0.08 to *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

**Sap** can be gathered from trees that yield logs by striking them or by collecting them from a built Sap Collector. **Sap** is used to upgrade different weapons by acting as a binding agent to hold certain materials to the weapon, such as feathers or teeth. Prior to update 0.53, it was used to repair structures. It is a primary ingredient used in permanent weapon upgrades.

The chances of receiving **sap** while cutting a tree down is based on chance. Weapons that are poor at cutting trees are often the best such as the machete. Though Sap Collectors are a far better method for harvesting sap. Only axe type weapons that cut trees can harvest sap.

## Trivia

* **Sap** use to be used in crafting blue paint and orange paint, however both paints were removed in v1.0.

## Notes

* Once you have stacked 10,000 sap, trees will no longer drop it when hit. You will still be able to harvest the sap from a sap collector, however the stack number will not increase in your inventory.
