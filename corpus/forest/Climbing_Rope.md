The **climbing rope** can be attached to certain buildings to allow climbing above or below certain areas. They were added to the game in update v0.10 to caves, though they were made buildable in update v0.13 of *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

The climbing rope can be found at the utility structures section of the survival guide, which can be used to climb up buildings that it is attached to.

The climbing rope can also be found in most caves, they often act as entrances to caves as well. They operate is the exact same fashion though they cannot be removed.

To use a climbing rope, press `E` at the bottom of the rope. Occasionally, the rope will be poking through the floor, the player may have to crouch and aim into the ground to see the climb icon. If the player holds the `SHIFT` key while going up or down the rope, they will be able to utilitise it faster. Holding shift whilst going down will cause the player to slide down the rope at a much faster pace. If they hold shift while going up, they will climb it faster but they will use more stamina. The following buildings have certain amount of anchors that the climbing rope or tree bridge can attach to:
Gazebo (1), Log Cabin (2), Platform (3), Tree Platform (4), Cliff Side Platform (5), Simple Stairs (6) and Basic Wall (7)

## Glitches

* When climbing down a rope (if you're entering a cave wait for the zone to load) press interact to let go of the rope, and spam interact as you fall. You should grab the rope again at the bottom before you hit the ground, negating any fall damage while descending even the longest ropes in a second or two.
