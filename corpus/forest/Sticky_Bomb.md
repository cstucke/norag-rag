**Sticky Bombs** are explosive weapon items which were added in v0.08d to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

A **sticky bomb** is an upgrade of a bomb; when it is thrown it sticks to what ever it hits first. It can be attached to cannibals and mutants, making it easier to hit them with explosives. It will not roll away from its target. It can also be *placed* on trees instead of throwing it near them.
