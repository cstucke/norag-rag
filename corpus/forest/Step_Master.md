# Step Master
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Step_Master
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74889

## Description

*Pass 50,000 steps*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Be aware that steps count only from the moment you get the Pedometer. It doesn't need to be held permanently since it is counting your steps even while in your inventory. Player cannot combine steps between saves.

### Requirements

walking 50,000 steps with Pedometer
