**Squirrels** are tiny passive animals that were added in update v0.11 to ***The Forest.***

## Gameplay

When killed, they give the player one small meat and one Squirrel Head.
As with most small animals, **squirrels** can be killed with a single hit by any weapon in the game.
**Squirrels** can be caught in animal traps though they are often not wanted by most players. Consider killing them before triggering the trap. A crafted bow or a modern bow is often the most effective.
Ironically, **squirrels** cannot climb trees.

## Locations

**Squirrels** can be found throughout most wooded areas or even open areas. They are not highly sought after animals. They seem to be American red squirrels.
