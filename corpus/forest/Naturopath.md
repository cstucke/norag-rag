## Description

*Craft 10 medicine items*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Find and gather all three of the herbs around the peninsula. Then craft a total of 10 Herbal Medicine and/or Herbal Medicine+. Only health mixes count towards the achievement.

### Requirements

Aloe Vera  
 Marigold  
 Coneflower\*  
  craft a total of 10 Herbal Medicine and/or Herbal Medicine+
