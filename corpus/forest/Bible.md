The **Bible** is a story item that can be encountered in a number of spots on the peninsula. It can be examined by the player, but can't be added to the inventory or the player's notes.

## Theory

**Bibles** can most often be found near the Christian missionaries camps, both in and near caves, along with crucifixes. One can also be found on the yacht, suggesting that the unknown yacht group are possibly Christians.
