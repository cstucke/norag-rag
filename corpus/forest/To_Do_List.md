# To Do List
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/To_Do_List
Categories: Survival Guide
Revision: 74514

The **to do list** is an in game list in the survival guide that lists some things the player needs to do. Things get added to the list when the player discovers something like a cave or finds an item like the passenger manifest. When an item on the list has been completed it gets crossed out. The list is not compulsory and it does not provide a reward once completed. The **to do list** is more of a guide to help players and remind players of what content there is to do.
