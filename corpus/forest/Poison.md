# Poison
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Poison
Categories: Stats, Gameplay
Revision: 74524

**Poison** is a damaging status effect that can affect animals and mutants.

*This article is about the status effect received from poisoned weapons. For the status effect caused by eating poisonous food, see Sickness*

## Gameplay

The effect occurs when a animal or mutant is hit by poison arrow or by a poison upgraded weapon. Players cannot be poisoned through normal means, even by other players.

Unlike fire, poison can be reapplied while it is active, resetting it's duration.

## Effects on Animals and Mutants

When a creature is poisoned, it will appear with a green tint over it.
All creatures suffer the following effects while poisoned:

* Animation speed is slowed by 20%. This affects both movement speed and attack speed.
* Damage against players is reduced by 37.5% (3/8ths)
* Damage against buildings is reduced by 50%
* Take damage over time

### Damage

Damage amounts and intervals differ slightly depending on the creature.

* Animals: 2 damage instantly, 2-3 damage after 0.5 seconds, then 2-3 damage every 5-6 seconds
* Mutants: 2-3 damage every 3 seconds
* Creepy Mutants: 1-3 damage every 3 seconds

### Duration

* Animals: 40-60 seconds
* Most Mutants: 30 seconds
* Cowman: 20 seconds

Duration is reset every time a creature is inflicted with poison.

## Effects on Players

In normal gameplay, the player cannot be poisoned by any means. Hitting oneself with a poison arrow, or having another player use a poison upgraded weapon will not poison the player. The only way to be poisoned is through the *poison* console command.

Poison has different effects on the player to animals and mutants. Here is a timeline of how the player is affected:  
*Note: All damage dealt by poison ignores Armor*

* Instant: Take 4 damage, receive Blood Infection
* 4 seconds: Take 2-3 damage, set **INTERVAL** to a random amount between 6-8 seconds
* Every **INTERVAL** seconds later: Take 2-3 damage

Poison on the player lasts 50-80 seconds, chosen randomly when the poison is inflicted.

## Trivia

* If the player has a PS4 controller connected (either by playing the PS4 version or using a controller on PC), the light on the controller will turn green when the player is poisoned, and turn blue when the poison wears off.
