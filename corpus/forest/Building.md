# Building
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Building
Categories: Pages with image sizes containing extra px, Gameplay
Revision: 74575

**Building** is a game play mechanic allowing the player to construct objects such as fires and shelters using accessible materials.

To build structures, open the Survival Guide by pressing `B`. After selecting a structure, the player can designate where to place the blueprint. While designating the position of the blueprint, use `R` to rotate and the use button (`E` by default) to place it. When building a custom structure, place the first point by pressing `left mouse button` and remove the last point by pressing `right mouse button`. After the blueprint is placed, materials must be assigned to construct the object.

Some structures require the blueprint to be placed and then modified prior to having materials assigned, such as creating a gate on a Defensive Wall.

A blueprint may be deconstructed at any point prior to having all materials assigned to it. Although pressing and holding `C` will erase the blueprint and recover approximately half of the assigned materials, some are non-recoverable, such as turtle shells, leaves, and pots.

Most damaged structures may be repaired using a crafted Repair Tool or Sap. Some custom structures may require additional materials to repair, such as the Custom Foundation.

## Fires

Fires may be used to cook meat, stews, boil polluted water with a pot, provide warmth, provide a light source, and/or to protect against or attract cannibals and mutants. They can be fueled with leaves and cash which extends their burn length. They can be difficult to maintain when it is raining. Fires will also damage players if they walk into them with the exception of the standing fire. Fires can be used to burn dead cannibals for bones & skulls. If heads are cooked, they'll turn into skulls. If arms or legs are cooked for too long, they'll turn into a bone. There are some fires located in caves that the player can utilize. Fires can be lit with the lighter, molotovs, lit tennis balls and fire arrows. They can also be lit by a leaf pile trap if it is placed on or in a fire. When lit, the leaf pile trap will cause a chain reaction and light the fires at the same time. Fire Thrower Cannibals fire attack can also light fires.

## Shelters

Shelters are one of the most important buildings in all of *The Forest* game. They are used for sleep which restores energy, and also for saving your progress. All shelters come with a house overlay icon which marks its location, it can be seen anywhere in the game world. This is advantageous to the player when adventuring, so they can always find their way home. The player can press `R` on this icon to change its color, or change it to a white color to switch the icon off. Most of the **shelters** protect against rain and keep players warm during the night. Like most of other buildings, **shelters** can be destroyed by cannibals and mutants. Currently there are six kinds of **shelters**. Two are listed under elevated structures, being the Tree House and Alpine Tree House, which both require a tree to attach to.

## Boats

**Boats** can be used to travel the waters quickly, to move materials, to be a mobile base, to avoid cannibals, mutants, crocodiles, sharks, and to aid the player in fishing more easily. They can be used when building a base on an island, since most storage buildings can be placed on them allowing for greater movement of needed materials. There are currently three different kinds of boats that the player is able to build: the Small Raft, the Large Raft (Requires two players to operate properly) and the House Boat. The three are similar, but still have their own pros and cons. Many useful structures can be built on boats, including storage containers, campfires, and skull lamps.
