All shelters and beds come with a house overlay icon.
All stick markers and log sled come with a flag overlay icon.
The overlay icon marks its location. It can be seen anywhere in the game world.

Overlay icon's colors can be :-

* yellow
* green
* light blue
* purplish pink (fixed for Log Sled)
* red-brown
* grey (disable)
