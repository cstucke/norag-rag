**Infobox: Custom Wall**
**Description**
- **Also known as:** Experimental Wall
- **Added in:** v0.11
- **Building Type:** Custom; Defensive
- **Upgradable:** No
- **Custom:** Yes
- **Variations:** Window; Door with lock
- **Attachments:** Custom Walls; Custom Roofs; Custom Floors; Rock Walls; Stick Fences; Bone Fences
- **Uses:** General Protection; Decoration; Custom Building; Defense
- **Negatives:** Difficult to stack as a pillar
- **Stores:** N/A
- **Stackable:** Yes
- **Buildable on:** Most surfaces; Top of House Boat
- **Durability:** Strong

**Custom Walls** are used primarily to place walls on custom floors and custom foundations. They can also stack on rock walls. Custom Roofs can be placed on top of **custom walls**.

## Building

| ∞ | **=** | ∞ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Custom Wall** |  | Log |

It costs 5 logs per section, regardless of whether its a wall, window or door.

## Trivia

* Custom walls were known as experimental walls when they were first introduced in v0.11. Their name was changed to **custom walls** in v0.31.
