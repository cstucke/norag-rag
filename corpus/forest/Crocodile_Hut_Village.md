The **Crocodile Hut Cannibal Village** is a cannibal built location.

## Gameplay

This village is location in between the river and the snow area. A stream runs through it causing it to look partially flooded. It is possible that cannibals may have abandoned it due to the high amounts of crocodiles, though this is speculation. Patrol routes pass through here fairly often. There are a number of Lookout Posts in the village, and in the river next to it. Some items can be found at the top of these posts. There are some items located on the ground around the village, though it is very limited. Players should avoid standing still and always be on the look out for crocodiles in this village. On normal and hard mode, there will always be 1-3 crocodiles in the village. If the player is feeling ambitious, they can attempt to get the suitcases out of the river, though this can be dangerous due to the crocodiles.
