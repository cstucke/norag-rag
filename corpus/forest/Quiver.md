**Quiver** is a craftable inventory storage item that was added in update v0.32 of ***The Forest***. It operates the same as the throwable rock bag, stick bag, and rock bag in that it permanently increases storage.

## Gameplay

The quiver increases the Arrow carry capacity by 20. This includes all types of arrows:  
 Arrow  
 Bone Arrow  
 Fire Arrow  
 Modern Arrow  
 Poison Arrow

Additional storage can be sought by building an Arrow Basket. They can be placed on boats.
