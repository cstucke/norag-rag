# Longest Wall
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Longest_Wall
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74732

## Description

*Build a super long wall*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

All you need to do is place the "blueprints" for your wall with 50+ segments. The player must make sure to make a Custom Wall as the Defensive Wall does not work towards this achievement.

### Requirements

Custom Wall
