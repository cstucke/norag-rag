**Chicory** is a harvestable plant, herb, and material item that was added in update v0.01 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Chicory** can be recognized by its light blue flowers. Similar to aloe, coneflower, and marigold, **chicory** will often spawn in groups on the forest floor. It can be crafted with coneflower to make energy mix, or crafted with coneflower and aloe to make energy mix +. If eaten alone, chicory will replenish a small amount of energy.

The player can only carry 10 chicory in his inventory at a time.

## Trivia

* Chicory is based on the real life plant, *Cichorium intybus*.
* There are currently no chicory seeds, which means chicory cannot currently be farmed in a small garden or a wall planter.

## Bugs

* In multiplayer, there is a chance that the same herb plant will appear differently for different players. For example, what appears as marigold for one player may appear to be aloe or chicory for another.
