**Rocks** are a multi purpose item that has been in the game since release.

## Gameplay

**Rocks** are an unlimited natural resource that can be found lying on the floor of the forest and the beaches. The player can carry up to five **rocks** in inventory at a time, 10 when having a rock bag. A **rock** may be picked up by pressing `E` near it.

**Rocks** can be found on the ground as well as Throwable Rocks; the small ones can be thrown to distract enemies or used in the Slingshot. The **rocks** in the environment respawn quickly after being picked up, they will spawn in random locations. The player may have to walk a short distance away to cause them to spawn. This works in both single, and multiplayer. Throwable Rocks can be dug up by hitting the ground with an equipped stick or upgraded stick.

Each player sees its own **rocks**. This can be seen in multiplayer, where a player can see **rocks** that no other players can.
