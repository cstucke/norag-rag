A **torso** is the midsection of a mutant after the arms, legs, and head have been removed. They will become covered in flies and eventually disappear unless stored in a log sled.

## Gameplay

A **Torso** can be burned for two bones per torso. If the arms or legs are attached, each will provide one bone per limb. The **Torso** can also be attached to custom effigies. They can be stored in log sleds until they are required to be used. Like bodies, **a torso** will expire if you don't use it. If you place it on a log sled, this will prevent it from expiring. You can also save and exit with a **torso** on a log sled and it will be there when you return.
