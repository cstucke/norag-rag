**Feathers** are material items that were added in v0.05 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Feathers** are located in one of the front pockets of your backpack in your inventory. You can also carry an unlimited supply of **feathers**.

**Feathers** are dropped from birds as they fly and can also be obtained by killing a **bird**, which will yield 3 - 5 **feathers**, depending on the **bird**. They will float in the air and can quickly become out of reach if not collected.

**Feathers** can also be obtained by building a birdhouse. It will passively collect **feathers** even while you're away from it. It can hold a max of 30. It works in a similar way to Sap Collectors. Birds will also land on it, so it can add a nice feature to your base.

As of v0.16, dead birds had a chance of spawning **feathers** when hit, but this feature only works with Geese in v1.12.
