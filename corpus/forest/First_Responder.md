# First Responder
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/First_Responder
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74692

## Description

*Revive one co-op player*

## Guide

Obtainable only in Multiplayer.

Revive one player in multiplayer mode, must be done before they die. Players who die to sharks or drowning cannot be revived.

### Requirements

Revive one other player
