**Falling** is a source of damage that will affect the player and cannibals when they drop from a high distance onto the ground. Falling is one of the few kinds of damage that bypasses armor and reduces the player's health directly.
Unlike when you are attacked by sharks, you will end up in a cave when you die from fall damage. Fall damage cannot be avoided
