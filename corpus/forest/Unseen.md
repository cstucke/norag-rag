## Description

*Make and wear stealth armour*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Chop down several bushes until you have 15 leaves, kill a lizard or crocodile and skin it, combine 15 leaves and one lizard skin then equip the stealth armor.

### Requirements

craft and equip Stealth Armor
