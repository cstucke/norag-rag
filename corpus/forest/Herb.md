# Herb
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Herb
Categories: Gameplay, Flora
Revision: 63271

**Herbs** are consumable material items that are used to make energy mixes and herbal medicines. They are also used as an ingredient for stews. In stews, they have their own classification, like meat and mushrooms. Aloe won't cure infection or sickness if it is used in a stew.

## Herb Types

Aloe Vera  
 Chicory  
 Coneflower  
 Marigold
