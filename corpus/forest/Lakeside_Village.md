The **Lakeside Cannibal Village** is a cannibal built location.

## Gameplay

This village ***is located right next to the large* lake**. It consists of several huts and a **cannibal** built fire in the center which has an old pot in the middle of it. There is cache 3 inside one of the **huts**. There are a number of **cloth racks** and a fair number of suitcases around. There is a Lookout Post within the **village**. There is a dirt pathway leading out of the **village** that leads to Cave 2 - Hanging Cave, this is the closest **entrance** to the modern axe. Around the lake is a large number of berry bushes and a capsized boat with 2-3 **ropes** on it. There are fish spots all around the **lake**.
