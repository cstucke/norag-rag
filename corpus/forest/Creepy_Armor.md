**Creepy armor** is an armor item that is obtained from skinning creepy mutants, it was added in update v1.0 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Creepy armor** can be obtained from skinning dead Virginias, Armsies, and Cowmen. Also, when killed, the Girl mutant will drop 3 pieces of **creepy armor** at its place of death. However, Mutant Babies do not contain **creepy armor**. It currently provides an armor rating of 80 per piece. Bone armor, the next strongest armor, provides 40 per piece. It was added as a reward for killing mutants. Prior to this update, there was no reward for killing mutants, aside from preventing them from killing you or destroying your buildings.
