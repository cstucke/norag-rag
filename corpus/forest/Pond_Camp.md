The **pond camp** is a small campsite consisting of 2 Blue Tents that can be used for sleeping and saving the game.

## Gameplay

It is located not very far from Cave 1 walk-in entrance. It can be found by going in a straight line after exiting the cave system, passing by some passengers and spotted by a small **fishing pond**.
