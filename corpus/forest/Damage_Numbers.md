# Damage Numbers
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Damage_Numbers

This page is to track how many hits it takes to kill each cannibal, so their health / stats can be recorded properly. Please do *not* update this page unless you have read the rules for proper addition *thoroughly*.

## Rules for Proper Addition

DO NOT add a cannibal if:

* you are not 100% sure how many times you hit it
* you are unsure if it was the same cannibal you hit and then got distracted and then hit again
* the cannibal is hit by another cannibal
* the cannibal runs over a fire
* if ANYTHING damages the cannibal besides you
* you are unsure of the difference between incapacitated and knocked down, for melee weapons
* you can't tell village scouts from regular male tribals from leaders, or you can't tell the two neuters apart

DO REMEMBER:

* gender of cannibal matters
* only masked cannibals should have serious discrepancies in their numbers (due to their three types)
