The **Armor Rack** is an armor storage building which was added in update v0.45 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **armor rack** is used to store armor that the player crafts, as the backpack can only hold 10 bone armor, 10 stealth, and 10 lizard armor, along with skins.  
 You can store up to ten pieces of armor, but bone armor is the only type of armor that can be placed on the head part, since it is the only type that can be used as a mask/hat.

It can store the following:  
 Lizard Skin  
 Stealth Armor  
 Bone Armor  
 Creepy Armor
