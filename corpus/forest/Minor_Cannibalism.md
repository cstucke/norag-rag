# Minor Cannibalism
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Minor_Cannibalism
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74696

## Description

*Eat an enemy*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Requires you to eat 2 arms and 2 legs. Can be cooked on a fire or possible stewed. Cutting limbs will reduce sanity by 1 per limb and eating limbs will reduce it 3 per limb. Dried limbs will not count.

### Requirements

eating 2 arms and 2 legs
