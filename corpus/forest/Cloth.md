**Cloth** is an item and crafting material that was added in update v0.01 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Cloth is commonly found inside suitcases, along the shores of beaches, in cannibal villages, from cloth crates in caves (notably Cave 5 - Submerged Cave), effigies, and potentially many other locations throughout the peninsula.

It is one of the most sought after items in the game and can be used for crafting many of the game's items and for some of the games buildings. The primary use for most players is crafting bone armor, molotovs, turning cloth into rope, or making fire upgrades for weapons.

Cloth can be multiple colors on the ground or in suitcases before being picked up. Regardless of what color it was on pickup, it will be added to the inventory as a red strip.
