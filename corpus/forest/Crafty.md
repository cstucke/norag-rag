## Description

*Craft 26 specific items*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

See Crafting.

### Requirements

crafting 26 specific items

Here is an alphabetic list of all known items that need to be crafted to the get the **crafty** achievement, as well as their crafting recipes. Over time, some of the previously required items have been either removed from counting towards this achievement or replaced. These items are working as of May 2019.
