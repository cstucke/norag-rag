The **cargo manifest** is a story item that was added in update v0.49 to *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

The **cargo manifest** is found on the western shore of the Peninsula. It's added to the notes section of the survival guide when picked up.   
Along with the logo on the sides of the red **shipping containers**, it's often the first clue the player gets to the presence of Sahara Therapeutics on the Peninsula, which plays a huge part in the story.

## Theory

As the writing on the **cargo manifest** is difficult to read, it is hard to determine what role the **cargo manifest** has in the game. It may be just a clue for the player regarding Sahara Therapeutics and that they had activity on the Peninsula. From what can be made out, there were apparently 12 shipping containers from Sahara aboard a cargo ship called the Spirit Melissa. Based on the fact that the destination of this cargo was the Peninsula (and the fact that only 7 of the containers are present), it seems these containers fell off ship during a storm of some kind, resulting in most of them falling in the waters off the coast while the other 5 arrived at the Lab. Based off the fact that most of the containers are empty, the cargo inside was either lost in the storm and drifted into the ocean or were taken/recovered by either the mutants or Sahara employees (most likely a mix of both).
