**Energy Mix** is a craftable food item that was added in update v0.08 in *The Forest*

## Gameplay

**Energy mix** can be used to restore stamina and energy in an emergency or when required. It can be assigned to a quick select slot and used during battle. You can only carry 5. There is a more powerful variety called Energy Mix+, which will restore twice as much stamina.
