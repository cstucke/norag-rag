**Seeds** are unique items in *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

You can plant **seeds** in a garden to grow plants like aloe or berries. **Seeds** can be obtained by picking up plants and flowers along the Peninsula. Seeds cannot be consumed as food, even in an emergency. Seeds cannot be grown in cave gardens, only mushrooms can, mushrooms don't use seeds, they use themselves to grow the mushroom. One mushroom can turn into 3 mushrooms with this. Seed loot odds 70% = 0, 30% = 1 (it was previously 50% = 0, 25% = 1, 25% = 2 in update v0.34)
