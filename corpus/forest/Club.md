The **Club** is a melee weapon that was added in update v0.01 though made usable in update v0.08 of ***The Forest***.

For the craftable version, see Crafted Club.

## Gameplay

**Clubs** can be looted from most male regular mutants, all male painted mutants and all leader mutants. It is decorated with body parts such as two hands, a dead snake wrapped around the grip and three claw-like objects in the mouth. In the previous version of the game, the decorations were slightly different, and included braided female hair along with a blue cloth piece.

It is a slow weapon, but deals significant damage to enemies. Along with the turtle shell and the Rusty Axe, the **Club** has the highest block level possible for a weapon. Using it to block will negate any damage from enemies' attacks, even from powerful mutants.
The **club** can receive upgrades as of v0.72.

## Trivia

* The player can carry both cannibal and crafted versions at the same time, since they are two separate weapons.
* It cannot be used to dismember dead corpses.
* All cannibals hold it with their left hand, although they'll also use their right barehand to hit their enemy.
