The **beach camp** is a campsite located on the beach. It is a common spot for saving and an easy spot to find an old pot.

## Gameplay

The **beach camp** is a common spot for players to save their game and collect the pot that respawns there. It is easy to find and the tent can be used to sleep and save unlimited times. It is on a patrol route however. The camp itself appears to have belonged to a blogger of some kind, as evidenced by the magazines, the proximity to the shore (arrived via boat most likely), and the laptop, which indicates a possible date for the camp being set up as either the late 2000s or early 2010s.
