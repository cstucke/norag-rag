The **land bridge** is a piece of land that separates the river from the sea. It is quite thick and has a sharp elevation that cannot be climbed. It is one of the most dangerous places to build in the game.

## Gameplay

The **land bridge** is used by mutants, who patrol backwards and forwards between the Beach Hut Village, Waterfall Village, and the Seaside Village. Due to this, the **land bridge** is an extremely dangerous place to build, though it can make for a great challenge.

## Place to build

Building on the **land bridge** is a viable build spot, as it does have a scenic view and it’s close to tortoises, crocodiles, lizards and rabbits. The player can wall off both sides which makes it a fairly easy place to defend. If the player builds walls and blocks the patrol routes, they could have a relatively peaceful game. If the walls are close to where the player is build their house, the walls may be attacked. The hardest part of building here is the lack of sticks and logs. Freestanding Ziplines can be used to bring in logs, though sticks are very minimal.
