The **Ceiling Skull Lamp** is a light source, decoration building that was made buildable in update v0.27 of ***The Forest***. They were added in v0.01 to caves though they were not buildable until v0.27.

## Gameplay

**Ceiling skull lamps** allow the player to hang a light source from the interior roof of a building or boat and some unique surfaces like cannibal huts. They can also be placed in windows of walls.
