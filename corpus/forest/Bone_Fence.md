**Bone fences** are custom buildings that were added in update v0.30 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

It can be found under the custom building section of the survival guide. **Bone fences** are a fence made of only bones. It serves as a defensive wall against enemies, but it can be destroyed. The fences can be placed like the defensive wall or rock wall and can also be autofilled on custom foundation , custom floors and custom roofs. Enemies can jump over one layer of fences but if you stack two or three layers, they won't be able to. This fence is twice as strong as the cheaper to make stick fence
