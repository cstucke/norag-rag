**Skinny pale cannibals**, also known as *Starving Pale Cannibals*, are small mutants with dark blue/grey skin, with completely white eyes, no body hair of any kind, no clothing or adornments.

## Gameplay

**Skinny pale cannibals** will often be in caves together from the beginning of the game. **Skinny pale cannibals** stand hunched over, and move in a more primitive, skittering fashion. They, alongside pale cannibals, are the only kind of mutants found in the caves. **Skinny pale cannibals** have the same complexion as pale cannibals.

After day 5, they start spawning on the surface, regularly travelling together with pale cannibals. They will begin to replace the standard skinny cannibals for the most part. Pale skinny cannibals are eventually replaced in most patrols by the Masked Skinny Cannibal after day 22, though they will still spawn as scouts for creepy mutant patrols.

In fact, the player can use this knowledge to their advantage; if pale skinny cannibals are spotted above ground after masked cannibals start spawning, then that means there are creepy mutants nearby.

## Trivia

* Like the Pale Cannibal, Masked Skinny Cannibal and Masked Cannibal, there is no female version of this mutant in the game.
