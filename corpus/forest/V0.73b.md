Version **0.73b** was a hotfix update released February 2, 2018.

## Additions, Changes, & Fixes

* Fixed sled storage not working after moving sled unless interacting with its push trigger
* Fixed sled impossible to let go with the “Take” button unless interacting with another trigger in world
* Fixed a case of getting disengaged from a sled, for example when falling off a cliff, not turning off everything properly and allowing to grab the sled again from a distance by holding the “Take” button
* Fixed modern arrows falling at player's feet when fired﻿
* Fixed an issue with autosave not functioning in the Dedicated Server.
