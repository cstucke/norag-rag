**Cave Science - Sahara Cave** is a temporary place-holder name for the cave located between the research lab and the elevator to the observatory. It is only accessible via the research lab, with a walk-in entrance. It features dozens of mummified corpses, perhaps those of Sahara employees who have fallen to the death obelisk. It requires the rebreather to swim through, and there are no enemies. It has one set of sacrifice doors.

## Speculation

The caves appear to have been recently explored, most likely just before the incident at the lab. These caves may have been in the early stages of being turned into labs to study the Power Obelisk. Evidence of this can be seen in the equipment scattered around (including papers and trash cans), the proximity of the caves to the new hallway and the business offices (which lead to the elevator), and the collapsed hallway at the end leading to a set of double doors.

The caves also house a variety of mummified corpses that appear in the more unique areas. How the mummies connect to the cannibals is unknown, as they were left alone after death rather than being eaten, suggesting their presence may pre-date the formation of the cannibal society. These bodies could possibly be part of the missionaries, as their poses and appearance are similar to the ones in Cave 5.
