The **Gazebo** is a custom building that was added in update v0.08 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

It serves no purpose other than decoration, as it can not be interacted with. Currently the **gazebo** requires the most sticks of any player made structure. The final amount of logs is determined whether it needs additional foundations and how high it is placed.

Due to the large resource requirement, it often takes a long time to create a **gazebo**. To speed up the construction of a **gazebo**, it is recommended that players make use of a log sled and a stick holder. Use of multiplayer can also speed up construction with teamwork.
