- **Obtained through:** Crafted
- **Equipable:** No
- **Consumable:** No
- **Storage:** Cannot be stored
- **Other Effects:** Increases small rock carrying capacity from 10 to 25

The **small rock bag** is a craftable inventory storage item that was added in update v0.71 of ***The Forest***. It allows the player to carry up to 15 additional small rocks. This can be extremely helpful when using the slingshot, as it more than doubles your slingshot ammo capacity. It operates the same as the quiver, stick bag and rock bag in that it permanently increases storage. Like the other bags, it cannot be removed from the inventory, placed on the crafting mat or shared via the metal tin tray.
