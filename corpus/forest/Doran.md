## Trivia

He is potentially the man who walks past in the camcorder tape Lunch with Megan. However, there is no evidence to suggest this and it is only a theory. In this video tape, the man has a different hairstyle and given the lack of variation in any of the game's 3D facial design he could easily be a different person with a reused character model.
