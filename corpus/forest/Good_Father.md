## Description

*Collect all robot pieces*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Once you have all the pieces, go into your Inventory and combine each piece to create the entire robot to trigger achievement.

### Locations

The **Toy** is separated into 6 parts, which are all located in caves.

| In-Game Name | Appearance | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Toy Head** |  | - Cave 2 - Hanging Cave. On top of a bloody table with hanging bodies and human hearts on the ground. The table is lit from above by flashlights and one or two cave branches away is the "snack and soda pit". |
| **Toy Torso** |  | - Cave 1 - Dead Cave. Near the drop down entrance (also known as the Katana cave). It is near some of Timmy's Drawings. |
| **Toy Left Arm** |  | - Cave 3 - Wet Cave. It is found sitting under two passengers who are hanging up. This cave entrance is near the river, across from the Riverside Village and the fertile lands. |
| **Toy Right Arm** |  | - Cave 7 - Chasm Cave. You will have to pass three skinny pale cannibals and one armsy. After destroying wooden planks with crosses, go straight forward to the end of the rock shelf (spot is lighted up by a skull candle). |
| **Toy Left Leg** |  | - Cave 5 - Submerged Cave. It is located near both the entrances, guarded by two skinny pale cannibals. |
| **Toy Right Leg** |  | - Cave 7 - Sinkhole Cave. Near the exit to the sinkhole, on the rock bridge. |
