The **stash** is an unimplemented building in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

To access the **stash**, the player will be required to use the *Ultimate Cheat Menu*. It can only be spawned, it cannot be built. It only obtainable in multiplayer. It can be found in the *spawn* section under the name `[MP]ItemStash_Built`
