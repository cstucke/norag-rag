**Bone Arrows** are a type of Arrow that were added in update v0.55 of ***The Forest***. They are exclusively used by the Crafted Bow and the Modern Bow.

## Gameplay

**Bone arrows** do 40% more damage than regular arrows.

**Bone arrows**, like all other **arrows**, are capable of head shots. This will most likely instantly kill a cannibal if it strikes them in the head.

On the PlayStation 4 version, the player must look down and press `R1` to change between **arrow types**, including **bone arrows**.

In the PC VR version, the player must look straight down and press the `Left Trigger` to change between **arrow** types.

## Known Bugs

* It is possible to shoot 2 **arrows** at once by pressing `Left Mouse Button` very fast after releasing first **arrow**.
* When being collected from a corpse, bone arrows will revert into normal arrows.
