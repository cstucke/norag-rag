# Water

Categories: Item, Consumable Item, Gameplay
Revision: 74933

**Water** is a crucial part of *The Forest* game-play.

## Uses of Water

* Quench thirst
* Wash blood off to avoid a blood infection
* Clean dirt and paint from the player's body
* Hide from the cannibals underwater, where they will not venture
* Swimming across deep bodies of water so that cannibals will give up the chase or drown themselves
* Source of fish and turtles

## Types of Water

There are three kinds of water in the game:

* Salt water, found in the ocean nearby. It is undrinkable.
* Polluted water, found in ponds. It is drinkable, but will damage the player's health.
* Fresh or clean water, created by catching rain in water collectors or by boiling polluted water in a pot. It is the preferred type of water, as it is drinkable and will not damage the player's health.

## Additional Information

When underwater, the player has a breath meter which slowly drains; when equipped with the rebreather and a full tank of air, the player can stay underwater for 300 seconds.
