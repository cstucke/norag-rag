The **stick frame** is a decoration based building that can be placed on most wall surfaces, it was added in update v0.63 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

It operates exactly the same as the bone frame, though uses sticks instead of bones. They do not serve a purpose other than being a decoration. It is intended for Timmy Drawings to be placed inside the frame, though this is extremely difficult to do. Once the drawing as been placed it cannot be removed, so you have one attempt. If aligning it is important, the player should consider saving first in case any mistakes are made.
