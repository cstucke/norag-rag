**Infobox: Rock Bag**
**Description**
- **Added in:** v0.43
- **Item type:** Item
- **Obtained through:** Crafted
- **Equipable:** No
- **Consumable:** No
- **Storage:** Cannot be stored
- **Other Effects:** Increase rock carrying capacity from 5 to 10

The **rock bag** is a craftable item added in v0.43 allowing the player to carry up to 5 extra rocks in inventory. This can be extremely helpful when building rock walls. The function of the **rock bag** is similar to the throwable rock bag, quiver, and stick bag and permanently increases cartable storage.
