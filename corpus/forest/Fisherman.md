## Description

*Catch fish with a trap*

## Guide

Currently only obtainable through Single player and as the host of a Multiplayer world.

There are small ponds all around the peninsula which contain fish. To complete this achievement, you will need to place an animal trap from the survival guide into a pond of water. The achievement triggers when the fish is caught, not when the fish is harvested from the trap.

The common animal trap is made on land which captures rabbits and lizards, however when placed into a pond of water, its automatically converted into a fish catching trap.

It's recommended to place a couple of traps as the fish will swim in sort of a linear path which may miss the trap if not placed in its path.
