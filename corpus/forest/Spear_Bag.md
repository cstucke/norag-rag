The **spear bag** is a craftable inventory storage item that was added in v1.0 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **spear bag** allows the player to carry more weak spears, up to a maximum of 5. It operates the same as the throwable rock bag, quiver, and stick bag in that it permanently increases storage. Currently, the player is still only able to carry 1 Incendiary Spear or Upgraded Spear.
