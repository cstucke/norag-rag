The **explosives holder** is a explosives storage structure that was added in update v0.13 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **explosives holder** works as storage which allows players to store all explosives, apart from flares. Currently you can store up to 10 items. here is a list of the items it can currently store:  
 Molotov  
 Bomb  
 Sticky Bomb  
 Head Bomb  
 Dynamite
