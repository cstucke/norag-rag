The **Freestanding Zipline** is a utility structure building that was added in update v0.63 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Logs can be sent down the zipline, additionally corpses and torso’s can be "carried" while using the zipline.
It has 2 gates, place either one high up and the other below and at distance, it will create a procedural rope in between that players can travel down. A player holding logs can press `C` to send one at a time down the zipline, but sending logs too quickly will cause them to drop to the floor.

* Ziplines are the fastest way to travel in the game.
* Pressing `E` or `Space` will let go of the zipline.
* Letting go of the zipline and falling into water is safe, falling onto land is not so safe.
* Regardless of which was placed first, the gate at the highest altitude will be the start of the zipline. You cannot ride it backwards.
* Cannibals and Mutants will attack zip lines if the player is close enough to them.
* Ziplines have a max length of 15 ropes. Mods can make the ziplines an unlimited length.
* Can be used to get to the bottom of the sinkhole by running it directly across the middle of it. Let go after approximately 6 seconds to land in the water at the center of the sinkhole.
* Carried items such as held live rabbits and animal heads will drop if you use the Zipline

## Known Bugs

* Freestanding Zipline blueprints can disappear and drop materials when rejoining servers. Best to complete them before hand.
