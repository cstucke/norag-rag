**Termination notice email** is a story item that was added in update v0.51 of ***The Forest***

## Gameplay

The **termination notice email** can be found in the Sahara Laboratory, towards the end of the game.

## Transcription of email

|  |
| --- |
| **|S| SaharaMail**   ---   **TERMINATION NOTICE**   2   ---   Nancy Helen (N.Helen@saharaco.com)  To: Mathew Cross (m.cross@saharaco.com)       **Hi Matt,**     **Your employment with Sahara Therapeutics is terminated for unethical and inappropriate use of company equipment.**     **Please submit your key card to security when you exit the building.**     **Please let us know if we can assist you during your transition.**               **Warmest Regards,**     **Nancy Helen,**     **Head of HR Sahara Therapeutics** |

## Theory

It appears that Dr. Mathew Cross was terminated, possible for misusing the obelisk. This most likely takes place after Camcorder Tape **Second Artifact Test**, with the concerning “Bring down a plane” email leading to his sudden termination. The singular email being in the printer suggests it was going to be copied and distributed around the lab (likely to notify personnel about Mathew being fired before replacing him) before whatever happened to the lab stopped it. **[MORE INFORMATION REQUIRED (possibly)]**
