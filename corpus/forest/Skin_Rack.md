The **skin rack** is a storage structure that was added in update v0.44 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **skin rack** allows the player to store raccoon skins, boar skins, rabbit furs, deer skins and lizard skins. The **skin rack** is divided in three segments, and any of the three skins can be placed anywhere on the rack. It can be found in the storage section of the survival guide. It can hold up to 18 skins/furs. The **skin rack** cannot be built on any of the boats, it appears it is one of the only storage buildings that suffers from this.
