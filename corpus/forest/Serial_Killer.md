# Serial Killer
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Serial_Killer
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74704

## Description

*Kill 100 cannibals*

## Guide

Currently only obtainable in Single player.

While most people will naturally complete this achievement by playing a longer single player game, the quickest way to complete it is by setting up some traps in a high traffic area and lighting a Bonfire to attract large groups of cannibals.

### Requirements

killing 100 cannibals
