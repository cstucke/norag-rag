## Viewing Stats

This table outlines all the stats and where they can be viewed. Some cannot be seen even with mods. (Mods refers to ModAPI and the ultimate cheat menu).
It may be possible that there are mods or console commands that allow the player to see some of these stats.

|  | Where stats can be viewed, if possible | | | |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stat | HUD | Stat Page | Console Commands | Mods | Description |
| Health | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Indicates health, when it reaches zero, you die |
| Energy | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Indicates energy, long term energy source |
| Stamina | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Indicates stamina, drains and refuels quickly |
| Armor | Some | Yes | No | Yes | Indicated in different colors, depending on the armor, prevents damage |
| Cold Armor | No | Yes | No | Yes | Helps prevent cold damage |
| Stealth | No | Yes | No | Yes | Helps the player remain undetected and with stealth attacks |
| Fullness | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Indicates how hungry the player is |
| Hydration | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Indicates how thirsty the player is |
| Sanity | No | Yes | No | Yes | Player is able to build effigies if **sanity** drops below 90% |
| Calories | No | Yes | Yes | No | Essential for raising strength and maintaining weight |
| Weight | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Nominal & starting **weight** is 205, **weight** has effects on strength & athleticism only; MIN: 155 & MAX is 305 |
| Strength | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Increases weapon damage |
| Athleticism | No | Yes | Yes | No | Increases sprint duration by lowering stamina usage per second and increases underwater duration (without rebreather) |
| Infection | No | Yes | No | Yes | Lowers **Strength** by 10%; Formula: 25% Chance when hitting a dead body or 25% Chance when hit physically while bloodied |
| Sick | No | Yes | No | Yes | Lowers **Fullness**, **Thirst**, **Health** and **Energy** gain from food by 10% |
| Cold | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Cold increases energy consumption and can cause damage is standing still for too long |
| Bloodied | Yes | Yes | No | No | Covered in blood, places player at risk of blood infection |
| Temperature | No | No | No | Yes | Hidden stat, if it becomes to low, the player becomes cold |
| Energized | Yes | No | No | No | Effect from restoring **energy** by sitting on furniture or listening to the Cassette Player |
| Poison | No | No | No | No | Effect occurs when a player is hit by a Poison Arrow; It damages the player over time for about 30 seconds |
| Death | Yes | No | No | No | Death occurs when you die... |
| Grey Zone | Yes | No | No | No | Occurs when the player is under 10 **health**, has bonus stat effects |
| Breath | Yes | No | No | No | Indicates how much breath a player has under water, increase with athleticism |
| Adrenaline Rush | No | No | No | No | Occurs when the player has very low **health**, will cause the player to gain 50 **stamina** instantly; Has a cool down of 2 minute |
