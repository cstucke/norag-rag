## Gameplay

There are several **bag**-type items in the ***The Forest***. You might be looking for:

* **Pouch**, which allows picking up berries (up to 30 of each) and mushrooms (up to 10 of each)
* **Quiver**, which increases arrow carrying capacity from 30 to 50
* **Rock Bag**, which increases rock carrying capacity from 5 to 10
* **Small Rock Bag**, which increases small rock carrying capacity from 10 to 25
* **Spear Bag**, which increases weak spear carrying capacity from 1 to 5
* **Stick Bag**, which increases stick carrying capacity from 10 to 20
* **Water Skin**, which allows for additional water storage
