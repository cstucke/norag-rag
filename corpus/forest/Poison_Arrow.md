**Poison arrows** are upgraded arrows added by v0.32 update of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Poison arrows** can be used with a crafted bow or a modern bow. The **poison arrow** will apply a damage over time poison effect, plus the original damage of an **arrow**. The effects do not stack.

Successfully poisoned enemies will get slowed movements and will do less damage for a short time. Poison lasts longer but does smaller damage than burning. [*citation needed*] If successfully poisoned, **enemies** skin will turn to green. Several shots may be necessary especially for mutants and strongest **cannibals** for the poison effect to apply.

They can be collected on dead corpses like **regular arrows** but their poison effect will be removed, needing the player to poison them again if they wish to.

It doesn't matter what poison berry or mushroom type you use to make **poison arrows**, it will create the same **arrow** regardless. Using **bone arrows** to craft **poison arrows** is a waste, it won't increase their damage or poison.

On the PlayStation 4 version, the player must look down and press `R1` to change between **arrow** types, including **poison arrows**.
