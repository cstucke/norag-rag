**Description**
- **Official Names:** Wet Cave
- **Player Given Names:** Cave 3
- **Added in:** v0.01

**Cave 3** has two entrances: one on the river-bottom at the west side of the Fertile Lands and one near the dirty white tents across from two cannibal structures nearby.

Much of the cave is underwater, similar to Cave 5 (which gives it its name; see below). The Rebreather and Climbing Axe are necessary to fully explore **Cave 3**. It is separated from **Cave 1** by a rope. Climbing the rope will grant access to the water areas, which are separated by a climbing wall that can be jumped down but not back up.

## Entrances

### Walking Entrance

Using this entrance is preferred, as the chainsaw can be retrieved without having to obtain the climbing axe or rebreather. Because of this, as well as the lack of creepy mutants, it is a good early cave to visit, along with the Dead Cave and Hanging Cave.
