## Description

*Knock down 6 or more trees with 1 explosive*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Find a big clump of trees, equip an explosive and throw it near the clump. A good location is the cache near Geese Lake, place an explosive into it and as well as opening the cache, you knock down enough trees for the achievement.

Dynamite is the best explosive choice as it has the largest explosion radius.

### Requirements

knocking down 6 or more trees with a single Dynamite, Bomb, Sticky Bomb or Head Bomb
