The **Autopsy Report** is a story item that was added in update v0.51 of ***The Forest***. The autopsy report first featured Megan Cross, but was later changed in v1.0 to Jessica Cross.

## Gameplay

The **Autopsy Report** can be found deep in Cave 2, surrounded by a cache of random items in the room where the Modern Axe spawns.

## Theory

It can most notably be found next to a bucket of red paint, implying that the Red Man has something to do with it. The **autopsy report** belongs to Jessica Cross, as noted at the top of the page. It says the victim died of multiple stab wounds and head trauma.
