The **Riverside Cannibal Village** is a cannibal built location. It is beside the river, hence its name.

## Gameplay

The **Riverside Village** is small and lacking in loot. Although there are few useful supplies, there is an outfit suitcase in one of the huts (added in v1.0). There are a fair number of cannibal effigies nearby, as well as a few canvas tents across the river and both Cave 3 - Wet Cave entrances in the vicinity.
