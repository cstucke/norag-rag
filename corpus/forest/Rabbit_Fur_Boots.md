**Rabbit Fur Boots** are wearable boots that were added in update v0.32 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

You can wear them on your feet to gain some protection from cold and slightly increase stealth. Unlike the stealth armor, the boots are not lost during combat. the boots don't affect stealth at all in hard survival mode. The stealth and cold armor you receive do not appear in the armor bar on the Hud. They can be seen on the stats page in the survival guide. They do not increase movement speed of the player.

You can only wear one pair of footwear at a time. **Rabbit Fur Boots** or Snowshoes.

**Rabbit Fur Boots** will still appear in your inventory, even when you're wearing them. To confirm, look at your feet.
