**Stealth Armor** is a wearable item that provides stealth and armor, it was added in update v0.12 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Stealth armor** works like lizard skin but also decreases your visibility in a similar way to the mud, with the advantage of being waterproof. When worn, parts of the player body will be covered with lizard skins with attached leaves.

**Stealth armor** is the only way to increase stealth to 100% on normal and hard mode. On hard survival mode, stealth benefits are reduced by 50% making it impossible to reach 100% stealth. It greatly assists the player in performing Stealth Kills, which kill Cannibals in one hit. Wearing Bone Armor reduces stealth.

Lizard Skin can be used as armor, but it does not provide stealth.

You can carry 10 pieces of **stealth armor** and 10 pieces of lizard skin at the same time.

## Known Bugs

* It's stated in the change-log that it was intended to require 1x tree sap, but it doesn't.
* When players load a saved game, the stealth armor will revert to lizard armor. (v0.12)
* The leaves may appear a bit oversized reducing the players view when using things like bows and lighters.(v0.15b)
* Occasionally if the stealth armor was broken the leaves will remain.
* Stealth armor cannot be added to the upper right leg of an armor rack. When scrolling, the armor selection skips over stealth. Adding it on through console commands results in the stealth armor piece floating in front of the rack. (v1.07)
