The **Lost Children of the Peninsula Tapes** are a decorative item that cannot be collected or interacted with, it was added in update v0.45 in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The tapes can only be found in the Hidden Stash Room, in Cave 2 where the Modern Axe is located.

## Theory

It appears to be a straight to VHS documentary into children going missing. The director is someone named Jake L. Heards. Mostly likely linked to the Milk Carton and the Siblings Missing Newspaper, which both mention missing children.
