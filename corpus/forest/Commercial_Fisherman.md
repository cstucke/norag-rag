# Commercial Fisherman
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Commercial_Fisherman
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74734

## Description

*Kill 6 or more fish with a stick of dynamite*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

The small ponds in the game only contain 3-4 fish at a time. To complete this achievement, you have to find fish spawns either in the large rivers (Geese Lake) or on the coastline. When you find a big enough group of fish, light a stick of dynamite and toss it near the water, as close to the group as possible. Careful, however, to not chuck it into the water, as it will extinguish the dynamite.

The best Game Mode for this is **Normal Mode** as it has 5x the amount of fish in a group compared to **Hard Survival**.

### Requirements

killing 6+ fish with a single stick of Dynamite
