**Soda** is an edible non-craftable hydrating item that was added in update v0.01 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Soda** is one of the most useful items for restoring hydration, energy and stamina. Multiple cans can be found inside the plane's interior along with booze. More can be found in locations previously inhabited by other humans, such as abandoned camps or the yacht. **Soda** can be found in crates deep in the caves and around some cannibal camps. **Soda** can also be found in Soda Crates, these are very common throughout the game world. The player can currently carry 10 **soda** at a time.

## Trivia

* This fictional brand of soda is called "FunDrink diet". Machines bearing the same logo can be found in the research lab.
  + The fictional brand in-game is probably based on "Sunkist" given the similarities seen on the coloring and design.
* Soda used to have many negative effects on gaining strength as well as putting on weight for the player. This system has been removed since v0.71 which has made it much more viable to consume.
