**Real! Magazine** is a collectible story item found in the cockpit of the plane. Upon interaction, it is picked up and added to the notes section of the survival guide. Only the cover is visible. It depicts a TV star with a young son, presumed to be Eric and Timmy Leblanc, and describes how his wife has recently died. The TV star is holding what appears to be the modern axe.
