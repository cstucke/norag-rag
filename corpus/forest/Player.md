The **player** is the character you play as in **The Forest**, it refers to both single player and multiplayer players.

## Singleplayer

In single player, you always play as Eric LeBlanc, the character cannot currently be changed within single player. The player has no dialogue throughout single player.

## Multiplayer

In multiplayer, the host almost always plays as Eric LeBlanc, while the other players are assigned a large array of different player models. These are all assigned at random. There are currently 3 races, these are the names listed in the game files:

* Caucasian
* African American
* Latino

Each one of these races has their own faces. Clothing has a much larger variety of possible differences including:

* Jackets
* Beanies
* Tuxedo's - Only if you have finished the game, assigned randomly
* Other assorted items of clothing
