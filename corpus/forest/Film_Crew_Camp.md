The **film crew camp** is a tent saturated location. It has links to the story. There are dead bodies, cameras and other objects to suggest so.

## Gameplay

The **film crew camp** has a larger, established camp with modern tents and film equipment scattered about, with scripts for a *Charles: The Survival Man*, which appears to be a script for a TV show or movie.
