The **Large Cabinet**, also known as the *Snack Cabinet*, is a storage building that was added in update v0.13 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **large cabinet**, much like its smaller cousin, the small cabinet, can be found on the second page of the storage section in the survival guide.
It provides space for up to 12 items of the ones named above. When facing the **large cabinet**, the item to be placed can be cycled through using the *Rotate* key (default key `R`).
After being placed, the **large cabinet** can be destroyed by hitting it repeatedly with a weapon of choice. As with other objects, it neither drops any resources nor its contents upon destruction.
