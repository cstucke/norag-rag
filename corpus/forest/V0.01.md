**v0.01** was the initial release of The Forest. It was made available on Steam on May 30th, 2014.

## Dev Notes

We have officially released on Steam!

http://store.steampowered.com/app/242760/

Enjoy

---

Thanks to everyone, we are currently the number one game on Steam!

As it's the first public alpha there are a lot of bugs for us to get through. Please send all bug reports to support@endnightgame.com . We will be setting up a Trello Board soon to make bug tracking easier.

Saving and loading seem to break in our latest build. We will try and do a hot fix out as soon as we can.
