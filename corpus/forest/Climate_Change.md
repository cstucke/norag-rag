## Description

*Cut down 100 trees*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Requires the player to cut down 100 trees using axes, the chainsaw, or explosives.

A good method to obtain this achievement the old fashioned way is to get yourself the warmsuit and modern axe and head up north to the Mountain Area. There are a number of smaller trees there that produce sticks rather than logs, yet still count towards the achievement. These trees will go down in just a couple of swings of the modern axe. Furthermore, since there are very few patrols there, you can cut down trees relatively unbothered.

### Requirements

axes, chainsaw or explosives

### Console Command Method

This achievement can be obtained by the use of Console Commands, here is the process after you've activated them (see Console Commands):

1. `additem 29` - this adds Bombs to your inventory
2. `itemhack on` - makes items endless
3. throw bombs everywhere, be careful not to kill yourself
4. `godmode on` - makes you invincible
