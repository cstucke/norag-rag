The **deadfall trap** is an advanced trap building which was added update v0.01 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **Deadfall Trap** is designed to kill cannibals and mutants by dropping a falling log on them when triggered. Like all advanced traps, it will not trigger when animals run through it. In previous updates, it was less effective against cannibals as they would often run through the trap without being killed, but this was greatly improved in v0.67 - it is now much more effective, though it can no longer be climbed upon. Its low material cost also makes it relatively easy to make in a hurry.

Deadfall traps instantly kill cannibals on all difficulties. On Normal difficulty, mutants also die in one hit; however, on Hard and Hard Survival, the first hit will severely weaken them instead of killing them, and another trap is required to kill them completely.

As of v0.68, resetting traps now requires to hold the “Take” action 3 seconds.

**Deadfall traps** do the exact same amount of damage as Happy birthday traps.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**+** Very cheap to make.  
**+** Can't be destroyed by cannibals.  
**+** Much larger area of effect than the Happy birthday trap.

**-** Cannibals running perpendicular to the trap can trigger it without dying.  
**-** One enemy can set off several traps at once.  
**-** Easy to set off accidentally.

## Trivia

* In very early versions of the game, the **deadfall trap** had downward spikes attached. They can be still seen in the Survival Guide image.
* In update v0.67, the **deadfall trap** was revamped to have a much large area of effect when the trap is triggered. It also had sticks added to the sides of the falling log to reflect the area of effect.
