**Snow Lake** is a lake in the mountain area. It is a large **lake**, and is a popular place for players to build near once they have acquired a warmsuit.

## Gameplay

This location offers one of the only water sources in the Mountain Area. It is very close to the grassy areas. There is a cache nearby with Gun Part 7 in it. A bit down the **stream** from the **Snow Lake** there is a Waterfall Village with a lot of different **items** and various **flora** and **fauna**.
