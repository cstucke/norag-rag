The Let's Camp Magazine is a decorative item that cannot be collected or interacted with, it was added to ***The Forest*** in v0.26.

## Gameplay

This is a magazine that can be found, though it cannot be collected. The textures make it easy enough to read.

The texts read, from top to bottom:

"12 Poisonous Spiders; What To Watch Out For", "Let's Camp!", "100 Camping Gadgets you should buy right away", "Bonfire Recipes", "Top Waterproof Sprays", "Lizards: A Beginners Guide", and "World's Best Water Collectors"

It can be found at the Beach Camp on the southeastern corner of the peninsula, as well as in the Film Crew Camp south of the Geese Lake.
