# Boy Scout
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Boy_Scout
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74701

## Description

*Use the compass*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Find the compass in Cave 2 (also known as the main camp cave). Once you go down the first rope, you will find two areas. The passage to the right will contain booze and suitcases. In the passage to the left, you will find coins on the ground near a wood cage. Follow the path toward the sound of running water. You will see a corpse in a patch of light that has snacks, the map, and the compass next to it. Pick up the compass and equip it from your inventory.

### Requirements

equip Compass
