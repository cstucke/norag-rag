The **log track** is a custom building added in update V1.10 to *The Forest*. It can be built once the schematics are collected from an underwater cave off the southern coast.

## Gameplay

The **log tracks** main use is for long-distance transportation of logs, but can also be used for sledding with the turtle shell. When starting a sled from the top of an angled **log track**, the sled will accelerate to its maximum speed very quickly, allowing for exit ramps, thrill rides, or other uses. A guard rail made of sticks is present on the sides to prevent the player from sliding off prematurely, possibly leading to an untimely death by falling.

Notably, **log tracks** also serve as cost-efficient and ideal ramps up steep inclines, as well as bridges across bodies of water. Unlike custom stairs, **log tracks** consist of multiple interconnected segments rather than a single ramp, meaning that they can turn corners, alternate size and slope, and provide greater flexibility. Unless built from near-maximum steepness, **log track** can be climbed on foot without needing to jump. Construction of **log track** is also more accurate, owing to the fact that custom stairs will acquire a slight offset to each step when completed, and the **log track** will not.
