The **paintbrush** is an examinable story item often found in conjunction with buckets of red paint.

## Gameplay

The paint is used by the Red Man, and the brush is assumed to be his method of applying the paint. This implies a level of sophistication beyond a cannibal, hinting that the Red Man is not a regular cannibal but someone who domesticated the cannibals. It can be found in multiple locations across the Peninsula, though it is much more infrequently found than red paint. It is only found in caves.

## Known Locations

Cave 2 - Hanging Cave, in the Hidden Stash Room  
 Cave 7 - Chasm Cave, on the way down to the bottom of the cave
