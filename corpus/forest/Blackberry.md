**Blackberries** are a collectable food and hydration item that were added in update v0.01 and made collectable in update v0.72 in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Blackberries** can be found all through the peninsula. They are one of the most useful food items as they provide hydration, fullness, and energy, just like blueberries. They only provide 1 calorie each. They do not spoil, making them useful for exploring deep into the caves. They grow on a sparsely leafed bush resembling a thorny brown collection of drooping vines. They cannot be used in stews. There are no seeds for **blackberries**, therefore they cannot be grown in a garden or wall planter. The hydration effect from **blackberries** and blueberries is halved in hard survival mode.
**blackberries** can be collected with the pouch, with a max carrying capacity of 30. **Blackberries** can be used for **Navigation** as they are always leaning to the **West**.
