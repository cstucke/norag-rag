## Description

*Share a weapon in MP*

## Guide

Obtainable only in Multiplayer.

Combine your metal tin tray with any weapon such as a crafted club on the crafting mat and equip it, then have another player collect the item from the tray.

### Requirements

Share a Weapon with other player using Metal Tin Tray
