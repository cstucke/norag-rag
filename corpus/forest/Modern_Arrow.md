**Modern arrows** are projectile weapons added in the v0.71 update of *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

**Modern arrows** are non craftable **arrows** that can be found deep in caves or from yellow crates. The **crates** are visually identical to the cases that flares are stored in. If you load a pre-v0.71 save file and have already opened these cases, the game will replace the already opened flare crates with unopened **modern arrow** crates, assuming the case doesn't stay as a flare case. The Modern Bow location has quite a few **modern arrows**; there is a **yellow crate** with one and there are some on a box right next to the **Modern Bow** itself. At the time of release, the developers stated in the patch notes that **modern arrows** are best used with the **Modern Bow**. It can be assumed that they said this because the combined stats of the **modern bow** and **modern arrow** currently provide the highest damage possible per bow shot (not counting poison/fire upgraded arrows).

On the PlayStation 4 version, the player must look down and press `R1` to change between **arrow** types, including **modern arrows**.

## Known Bugs

* It is possible to shoot 2 **arrows** at once by pressing `Left Mouse Button` very fast after releasing first **arrow**.
* If used to craft fire arrows or poison arrows, **modern arrows** will turn into normal fire arrows, and when retrieved from an enemy's corpse will revert into normal arrows.
