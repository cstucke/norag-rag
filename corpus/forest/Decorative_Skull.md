The **decorative skull**, also known as *skull wall decoration* or *skull decoration*, is a decorative building that was added in update v0.15 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The player can access from the decorations tab of the survival guide. It places a human skull on a stick as a decoration. It doesn't provide any light or specific purpose, it is only used for decoration. They can be placed in a straight line to give a fairly cool look. They are similar to head trophies though they skull can not be change for a head or some other item.
