**Outfits** are wearable items that change the appearance of the player's clothing; they were added as interchangeable items in update v1.0 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

There are several **outfits** the player can find.
Most clothing outfits are determined by randomized pickup rolls, which will grant different **outfit** combinations (e.g., green hoodie and camo jeans) from specific suitcases. A few special suitcases in specific locations will only offer a particular outfit associated with the environment/former owners of the luggage (e.g., the tennis outfit may only be obtained from the corpse of a tennis player or a particular bag near the bodies of several tennis players).

It is not clear whether outfits have any effect other than changing the superficial appearance of the player character. Wearing camo or neutral tones does not seem to add to a player's stealth, and the coverage of clothing does not seem to impact warmth (i.e., a character in an outfit with a t-shirt will become cold just as quickly as a player in a hoodie).

The player can carry up to 5 different outfits that can be changed in the inventory.

## Trivia

The Pattern and Colors found on the Suitcases that hold the outfits seem to take heavy inspiration from the real life French luxury company Louis Vuitton. On close examination, the patterns and color schemes share a lot of similarities along with the fact that Louis Vuitton sells suitcases.
