The **Artifact cave** is only accessible if the player chooses to shut down the The Power Obelisk. After shutting down the device, an elevator to the **Artifact cave** will be opened. After going down to the cave system, the player can't return to the Observatory. The top portion of the cave appears to have been used to store various items for the facility, while the lower areas were most likely being mapped out by cavers just before the incident at the Lab.
