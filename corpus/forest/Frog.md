**Infobox: Frog**
**Details**
- **Also known as:** Toad
- **Types:** None (invisible)
- **Location(s):** Ponds and lakes
- **Items:** None
- **Notes:** No moving ingame model

**Frogs** are merely decorative creatures that can be heard, but not seen, around lakes and ponds.

**Frogs** are however visibly present in a certain type of cannibal effigy, which can often be found around player bases.

## Knows Bugs

Frogs may have become invisible unintentionally, because the patch-notes never mentioned anything about it.
