The **machete** is a hand held knife-like tool and weapon added in update v0.63 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Like the Plane axe, the **machete** will chop down trees in 17 hits (9 for the modern axe, 13 for the crafted axe and the rusty axe). It can also dismember dead bodies.

The **machete** can cut stick trees, bushes, shrubs in one hit. This allows for fast stick gathering though greatly diminishes the amount of leaves you will harvest.

The **machete** has very short range. Almost all other melee weapons in the game have longer range than the **machete**. Even Bones and Turtle Shells have longer range. Longer range will allow you to create more distance between enemies and allow you to hit more enemies at the same time.

Despite the block power being listed as 0, the **machete** does block attacks, equivalent to the Katana.

The **machete** can be upgraded.

The **machete** cannot receive fire upgrades.

## Location

The **Machete** is one of the most difficult items to obtain in the game, being found at the bottom of the sinkhole, next to the crashed helicopter, where there is a large number of enemies.
