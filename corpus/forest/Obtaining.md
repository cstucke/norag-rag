# Obtaining
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Obtaining
Categories: Gameplay
Revision: 72336

**Obtaining** refers to the manner that the player can collect items.

## Collecting

Collecting refers to simply collecting an item. There is no process involved other using the interact button.

## Cooking

Cooking means placing items on a fire in a process to obtain the item.

## Crafting

Crafting means obtained from crafting.

## Cheating

Cheating means obtaining through console commands or mods.

## Farming

Farming means obtaining items from gardens or wall planters.

## Foraging

Foraging is a method a player uses to collect leaves, berries, and mushrooms.

## Hunting

Hunting means killing animals and obtaining items from them.

## Looting

Looting means killing enemies and obtaining items from them.

## Skinning

Skinning is the method the player may use to remove skins or other parts from dead animals. There are currently 6 animals that may be skinned, including:

* Lizard - Provides a lizard skin
* Rabbit - Provides a rabbit fur
* Deer - Provides a deer skin
* Crocodile - Provides 4 lizard skins
* Turtle - Provides a turtle shell
* Tortoise - Provides a turtle shell
* Boar - Provides a boar skin
* Raccoon - Provides a raccoon skin
