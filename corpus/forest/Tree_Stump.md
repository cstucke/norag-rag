# Tree Stump
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_Stump
Categories: Item, Flora
Revision: 72421

The **tree stump** will appear when a tree is cut down. All axes and melee weapons can break **tree stumps**. The chainsaw is currently the best tool for breaking **tree stump**s. When a **tree stump** is destroyed, it cannot grow back without the use mods or possibly console commands, though the commands for doing so are currently unknown. If *Tree Regrowing Mode* is on, 10% of tree stumps will grow into trees every time the player sleeps. The trees that are chosen to be regrown are assigned at random. If you have built over a tree stump, it may regrow through your buildings. Attacking a **tree stump** from different angles will change the amount of hits needed to break it. Attacking from the top with a plane axe will take 11 hits to break. Attacking from the sides will take 6 hits to break with the plane axe. Strength affects the amount of hits needed to break a **tree stump**.

## Known Bugs

* Sometimes tree stumps will become indestructible. Logging out and reopening the game usually fixes this.
