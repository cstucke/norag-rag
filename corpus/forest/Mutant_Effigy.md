# Mutant Effigy
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Mutant_Effigy
Categories: Pages with image sizes containing extra px, Building
Revision: 74239

This page is about the structures made by cannibals. For the various structures built by the player, see Effigy.

**Cannibal Effigies** are similar to the player-built effigies, and they are often built near the player or their base to scare them away or warn them. They can be found made in caves, villages, in hunting grounds, and at random places around the map. There are many different effigies that they can and will make, some for very specific reasons. Examples; the effigies that have a skinny pole with a woman's face strung to the top, are markers, with the face facing the place marked. They usually mark villages and sometimes caves, but sometimes the cannibals will build one near the players base, making the effigy face the base, Oddly, it seems that the cannibals will sacrifice one of their own, and place three of these effigies around the sacrificed cannibal, this can be seen all around the island, particularly on the northern half of the island. Another pattern is that cannibals will build a torso effigy with skulls stuffed inside, with one basic skull effigy on each side, these are built next to the players base. You cannot light effigies made by cannibals, but you can destroy them and gather their parts (cloth, skulls, sticks, and rocks) that are always dropped once destroyed.

## Overview

The cannibals also have different types of grotesque effigies, which can often be found along the shorelines and beaches but also in other locations all over the island. Cannibal effigies can be destroyed since v0.08D. There are at least seven different types of effigies, which can be unlocked in the "Effigies" Tab by getting below 90% Sanity at any point. The "Effigies" tab is a click button at the bottom of the first page of the "To Do" list marked effigies that takes you to the effigy pages.

## Cross Effigy

* **Description**: It is made of thirteen sticks which form the bottom half of a circle. Seven of the sticks are pointing towards a human skull sitting at the top to represent the upper half of the circle. Two shoes, a pair of sunglasses, and two umbrellas are also attached to it. Called **Umbrella Effigy** in the **patch notes**.
* **Locations**: Can be found at the ritual site (might have different location each time or multiple exist). Appears near the player's camp.

## Female Head Effigy

* **Description**: Two wooden circles affixed vertically around the head of a human woman, which is attached to her by her knotted hair pulled taut.

It may be important to note that the effigy itself looks like the symbol for "Venus" or "woman" of which she is always blonde, meaning that it may serve a religious purpose. It could also represent the symbol for "sun" as the islands natives can be seen on beaches performing some ritual worship.

* **Locations**: Often found along the shorelines and beaches.

## Boar Skull Effigy

* **Description**:The skull of a boar on a long vertical stick. It cannot be destroyed therefore it contains no materials
* **Locations**:Located at the east coast and at the southernmost hut in the main cannibal village
* **Resources when destroyed**: N/A This is the only effigy that cannot be destroyed.

## Fresh Human Effigies

These are possible effigies made out of humans and passengers. They will stay fresh as the first day through the whole player's game experience, not even attracting flies. Some passenger effigies will have equipment such as weapons, i.e. Katana. This weapon can be found in the body of passenger with the canvas of his tattoo that was apparently flayed from his forearm.
