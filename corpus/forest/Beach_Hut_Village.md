The **Beach Hut Cannibal Village** is a cannibal built location.

## Gameplay

The **Beach Hut Cannibal Village** is a number of beach huts spread far across the beach. It is an enemy spawn location and is patrolled often by enemies. It also holds the largest amount of dead sharks on the Peninsula.
