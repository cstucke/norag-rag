**Infobox: Cassette Tape**
**Description**
- **Also known as:** Tape; Music Tape
- **Added in:** v0.02
- **Item type:** Tool
- **Obtained through:** Collecting
- **Equipable:** No
- **Storage:** None
**Statistics**
- **Other Effects:** Used in Cassette Player
- **Max. Stack:** 5 Tapes, 1 of each

**Cassette Tapes** are used with the Cassette Player to play music, which increases energy and sanity, though it can attract enemies. It was added in update v0.02 of ***The Forest***.

# Gameplay

**Cassette Tapes** are necessary for the Cassette Player to work. Simply add a **Cassette Tape** and the Cassette Player to the crafting mat to add or change the tape.

# Cassette Tapes

There are currently 5 cassettes in the world. Some have duplicates in that they can be found in multiple places:

### Cassette 1

**Fatal Request - "Stronghold"**  
 - On the Yacht with the Cassette player.

### Cassette 2

**The Slaps - "No Conscience"**  
 - It spawns at the end of Cave 4

### Cassette 3

**Frankie Millionaire - "Do It"**  
 - Cave 10, near the cowman and the boat.

### Cassette 4

**Wolf on Fire - "The Haunting"**  
 - It spawns in Cave 2 in a large open room in a corner that can be found by skull lanterns and it is near mining hats.

### Cassette 5

**Rick Gold - "Strength, Ability and Wisdom"**  
 - On the Beachside Grave.  
 - Also spawns in Cave 1 in the mouth of a decapitated head on the path down to the Latin Paper.
