# A Way To Survive!
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/A_Way_To_Survive!
Categories: Guide
Revision: 48660

1. Gather supplies.
  
2. Find a safe location.
  
3. Set up a small camp of a hunting shelter, a basic fire and standing fire around the perimeter.
  
4. Build defensive walls around the camp and use collected rope for an entrance trap.
  
5. Gather up storage units and get resources together.
  
6. Create a field of traps across the cut down tree stumps as well as effigies and more standing fires.
  
7. Build any necessary or helpful equipment such as animal traps for food and water collectors for water.
  
8. Make spare time for food, water and supply runs through the woods or beaches.
  
9. Always beware of cannibal effigies or cannibals themselves as well as mutants.
  
10. Have fun of course it's not like it's a horror game or anything.
