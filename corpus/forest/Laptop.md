**Statistics**
- **Other Effects:** Contains a Circuit Board
- **Max. Stack:** 0

**Laptops** are decorative items and can be found around the peninsula, but mostly in the caves, at cannibal villages and at abandoned camps.  
They can't be used directly, but laptops can be destroyed with one hit of any weapon to reveal one circuit board.

## Trivia

* **Laptops** were added to the game in v0.03. However they couldn't be broken and didn't have items in them until v0.20.
