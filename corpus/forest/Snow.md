# Snow
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Snow
Categories: Environment
Revision: 74467

**Snow** is an alternate type of precipitation in *The Forest*, experienced in the mountain area only. It lacks lightning and thunder, though it still further exacerbates a cold condition. It does rain in the snow areas, though it appears as snow particles instead of rain. This will fill up water collectors and old pots with water.

## Effects

* Entering **snow** areas immediately drops the players temperature, making them cold
* Movement speed is reduced by 15% when walking on **snow**, snowshoes can increase your movement speed.
* Entering or leaving **snow** areas immediately reloads all Rock materials. This can be utilized to quickly fill a player's Rock Bag by collecting rocks near the biome border.
