**Energy Mix+** is an craftable food item that was added in update v0.43 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Energy Mix+** can be crafted using chicory, coneflower and aloe. It restores your energy to full when consumed and also restores 60 stamina. It provides more stamina than energy mix at the cost of one aloe. It can be assigned to a quick select slot for use in emergencies or when convenient.
