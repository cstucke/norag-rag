**Hairspray** is an fire emitting weapon when used in conjunction with the lighter, it was added in update v1.0 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

* It can be obtained in caves or suitcases
* When equipped it can be used by holding the attack button.
* When using the hairspray and having the lighter equipped, it ignites the spray and acts like a flamethrower.
* Hairspray has a rather limited capacity with a burn time of 15 seconds and should only be used if necessary.
* On close distance, it can ignite bushes, enemies, corpses and animals. Buildings are not affected in any way.

## Location

Hairspray can be found in caves and suitcases.  
 Beach Hut Village, in the far end north hut, in a suitcase  
 Waterfall Village, in one of the huts  
 Cave 5, near the entrance.  
 Cave 2, at the far ends of the cave.  
 Cave 7, in a suitcase next to the tennis racket

## Known Bugs

* Does not emit light when used with lighter.
* Emptying an equipped **Hairspray** sometimes also empties surrounding **Hairspray** cans on the floor.
* It will destroy already burning Basic Fire, Fire Pit and Bonfire
