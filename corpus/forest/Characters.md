# Characters
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Characters
Categories: Story
Revision: 74848

Characters are part of the story in *The Forest*. Many of these pages contain spoilers. Here is a list of the following characters and groups in or mentioned in the game:

* Doran - African American gentleman seen in camcorder tapes and at the end game
* Dr. Mathew Cross, PhD - Main antagonist, former head of the Jarius Project and pediatric geneticist
* Eric LeBlanc - Main protagonist
* Megan Cross - Daughter of Dr. Mathew Cross and Jessica Cross
* Jessica Cross - Wife of Dr. Mathew Cross and mother of Megan Cross
* Humans - Non cannibal and mutant creatures
* Obelisk Victim - Victims of the Obelisk
* Passengers - Passengers within the game, and an extensive guide to locating them
* Player - Regarding players changes, effects, clothing, and multiplayer. Doesn't contain spoilers
* Sam Sounde - Mentioned author of a number of books in the game
* Timmy - Main protagonists son

## Mentioned Characters

These characters are only mentioned in the game, mostly on story items. They could have an expanded role in the story in the future:

* **Allan Balm** - Investigator on the Autopsy Report.
* **Karl Thomas Peterson** - The name mentioned on the Keycard. It is difficult to see and the picture cannot be made out.
* **Zachery** - He is the missing child on the Milk Carton.
* **Sashi Bondi** - An employee of Sahara Therapeutics. Dr. Mathew Cross and her had an email exchange which can be seen on the Bring Down A Plane Email.
* **Nancy Helen** - Head of HR at Sahara Therapeutics. She sent the Termination Notice Email to Dr. Mathew Cross.
* **Brian Rits** - He wrote the article in the Siblings Missing Newspaper.
* **Charles Lai** - He wrote the script for Charles: The Survival Man. His name can be seen on the front. It's not an item that can be collected.
* **Jake L Heards** - He appears to be the creator of a documentary/movie. His name is seen on the Lost Children of the Peninsula tapes that can be found in the Hidden Stash Room. They are items that cannot be collected.
