# Sick
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Sick
Categories: Stats
Revision: 72378

**Sick**, is also known as food poisoning, is a temporary negative stat that the player can obtain.

## Gameplay

**Sickness** can only be viewed on the stats page of the Survival Guide. The game will not advise the player that they are **sick** on the HUD. **Sickness** has 20% chance of kicking in when getting damage from food. It lowers fullness/thirst/health/energy regeneration from consuming edible items. In Hard Survival Mode, It's easier to get sick or infected and there are greater penalties from sickness and infection.

## Causes of Sickness

There are a number of things that can cause sickness, though it's mostly consumables. Here is a list of what can make the player **sick**:

Poison Upgrade - Being hit by a poison upgraded weapon, can only happen in multiplayer, it has a 100% chance of making the player **sick** and they will also be poisoned  
 Poison Arrow - Being hit by a poison arrow, can generally only happen in multiplayer (unless the player is able to hit themselves with the arrow), it has a 100% chance of making the player **sick** and they will also be poisoned  
Consumables has a chance of making the player **sick**:  
 Snowberries  
 Twinberries  
 Arm  
 Head  
 Amanita Mushroom  
 Jack Mushroom  
 Leg  
 Spoiled Fish Meat  
 Spoiled Meat  
 Spoiled Lizard Meat  
 Spoiled Oysters  
 Spoiled Rabbit Meat  
 Spoiled Small Meat  
 Polluted Water - This includes polluted water from old pots, waterskins, or drinking from water sources

## Curing Sickness

**Sickness** can be cured by the following:

* Consuming Aloe
* Sleeping

## Effect and Formula

Effect:

* Lowers Fullness, Thirst, Health and Energy gain from food by 10%

Formula:

* 20% Chance when eating spoilt food
* 100% Chance when receiving poison arrow
