**Docks** are water based buildings that act like bridges, they were added in update v0.40 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Docks** are buildings that can only be placed on water or very close to water. They can be used as bridges though they are quite costly. It is possible for the player to anchor a House Boat to **docks** (check gallery). All other Boats can not be moored at this stage, this includes Small Raft and Large Raft.
It can be quite difficult to get the House Boat into position to do this. When it is in the correct position, to anchor the boat, aim at one of the pillars on the **dock**, a letter `E` should appear. Hold down `E` for approximately 2–3 seconds to anchor the House Boat. The boat will no longer be able to be moved until the player removes the anchor.

If a **dock** is destroyed while a House Boat is attached, it does not destroy the House Boat. It can still be used.
