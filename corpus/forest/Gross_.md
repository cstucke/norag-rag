## Description

*Drink from the water cooler that has the human head inside it*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

The cooler is in the Sahara Laboratory. You need to go through the second access door after you take out the Boss Mutant. Crawl up through the rock cave and swim under, you will need to have rebreather. The cooler with the head in it will be in the office on the right side of the hallway before you get to the elevator that take you to the 2nd Artifact.

### Requirements

drinking from the specific **water cooler**  
 Rebreather to access the location  
 Climbing Axe to access the location  
  Keycard 1 and Keycard 2 to access the location
