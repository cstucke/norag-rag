# Big Spender
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Spender
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74712

## Description

*Buy a soda and candy from vending machine*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

You must buy a Soda and Snack from the Vending Machines in the Sahara Laboratory. To access the **Lab**, the player must go through the Vault Door, located at the end of Cave HC at the bottom of the sinkhole.

### Requirements

buying Soda from Vending Machine  
 buying Snack from Vending Machine  
 Rebreather to access the location  
 Climbing Axe to access the location  
 Keycard to access the location
