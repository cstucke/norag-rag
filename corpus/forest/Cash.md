**Cash** is a material item found in The Forest.

## Gameplay

Their purpose currently is a substitute for leaves when building and feeding fires. They can be found in suitcases, inside caves, and on the ground surrounding cannibal camps and other points of interest.

They can also be obtained by beating hanging dead bodies using most weapons and tools.

## Trivia

* **Cash** are all $100 bills, despite their name.
* **Cash** icon are displayed when you are using vending machine although it doesn't use them, instead coins are used when buying the sodas and snacks.
