# Splatter
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Splatter
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74875

## Description

*Killed downed enemy with rock*

## Guide

Currently only obtainable through Single player and as the host of a Multiplayer world.

To complete this achievement, you must knock down alive cannibal and kill it using an equipped rock (not the small rock) while the cannibal is on the ground. Cannibals fall down faster in normal mode, so it is easier to achieve it on that difficulty. Once the enemy is on the ground, pull out your rock and bash their head with a downward strike.

### Requirements

kill downed enemy using Rock
