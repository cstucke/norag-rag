# Passenger
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Passenger
Categories: Story, Creature, Achievement
Revision: 74666

**Passengers** are the other occupants that were traveling on Coupon Air Flight 111 along with the protagonist. After crashing in the forest, the player is able to see the corpses of a few of them around the crash site, primarily the corpse of the woman where the Plane Axe is located, possibly the hostess. Many passengers may also be found hanging from the top of the caves where many of the cannibals reside in, cannibal villages, out the front of caves etc. It is unknown at this point in time if any living passengers will be present in the future, as the player may encounter camps and signs of life scattered around the forest. It can be assumed that at least 4 passengers are tennis players because 8 dead passengers can be found in the large mutant camp and in a cave who are wearing tennis gear, and they are posed with rackets and tennis balls.

## Console Command Method

1. In the main menu type in `developermodeon`
2. Start a game, a new one in this example
3. Collect the Passenger Manifest from the back of the plane
4. Press `F1` and type `findpassenger 1`
5. Press `F1` again - This opens the console
6. `UP ARROW` - This duplicates the last command
7. `DOWN ARROW` - This goes to the end of the command instead of the start.
8. `BACKSPACE` - Remove the numbers
9. Then type in the new numbers.
10. Rinse and repeat

## Known Bugs

* The names don't match the passengers appearance (see Japanese passengers), this might be texture issue
* One of the pilots found in Cave 5, leaning over a seat isn't counted as a passenger. Though is counted towards the old 130 passenger system. It appears this hasn't been updated or its yet to be implemented.

## Trivia

* Previously, there were around 130 passengers the player could find, but as of v0.28, the passenger count has been limited to 43 passengers. Although, many of them won't be recognized since their body is cut off in half or is too damaged.
