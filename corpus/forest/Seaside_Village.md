The **Seaside Cannibal Village** is a cannibal built location, it is near the sea and land bridge.

## Gameplay

The **Seaside Cannibal Village** contains two huts and is often patrolled by cannibals. It doesn't have many items of value except for two ropes. It does have some suitcase nearby, though on hard survival mode there may be none at all. The Cave 10 - Waterfall Cave climbing entrance is very close to the **village**.
