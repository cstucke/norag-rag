The **Crossbow** is a ranged weapon that utilizes crossbow bolts as ammunition, it was added to ***The Forest*** in v1.10.

## Gameplay

The crossbow is non-craftable, accurate, ranged and high damage weapon; 2 hits to body or one for headshots to kill cannibals.

Uses non-craftable bolts as ammunition. Some bolts can be found in the cave along with the crossbow. Also can be found stuck in the side of a broken ship on the island near the cave, at the top of a cannibal tower in a small village next to a pond. Bolts are capped of 10.

Bolts are gravity affected. It is easier to aim the closer you are to the target. Distant targets require you to aim a bit higher to be able to hit them.

There is a 1 in 3 chance for bolts to disappear after being shot, so you may not be able to retrieve them back. As with the Crafted Bow and the Modern Bow.

## Location

It can be found in the Shipwreck Cave, under the Ship Drawing and next to the first schematic.
