The **Upgraded Stick** (formerly the *Fire Torch*, still commonly known simply as the *torch*) is a craftable tool, light source and melee weapon that was added in update v0.05 of *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

The main purpose of the **Upgraded Stick** is to be used as a fire torch. It can be set on fire with the lighter to create a useful light source. However, it may be possible to see further in the dark without it lit. A burning torch will also negate the cold and wet status. Once crafted, it will become a separate tool from regular sticks. Collecting any clue item or equipping any other weapon will put out the fire.

As a weapon, the **Upgraded Stick** only deals little damage to enemies, but is quite effective to block and can be used to set enemies on fire. It swings very fast, and consumes little stamina, making a fairly good gathering (especially for teeth) and hunting tool. It can also be upgraded.

When stabbing the ground with it, the player has a small chance to reveal a small rock or some coins. Because of this, using the **upgraded stick** to open suitcases has a chance of revealing coins as well as opening the suitcase.
