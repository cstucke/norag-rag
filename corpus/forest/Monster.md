## Description

*Kill a bunny*

## Guide

Currently, this achievement can only be successfully done in single player mode.

The quickest way to complete this achievement is to start a normal game, once you find a rabbit, they can be killed easiest by using a crafted bow, slingshot or small rock, but you can also catch them with any kind of spear (you can attack low ground while running without stopping, unlike with other melee weapons) or other methods. Animal Traps can make this easier.

### Requirements

killing a Rabbit
