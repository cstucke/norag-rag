The **cache photos** are story items that were added in update v0.49 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **cache photos** are a set of 8 faded polaroids (resembling ones from the 1930s) revealing the locations of the flintlock pistol parts and their caches scattered across the peninsula. Each is labeled *"Dig Location #<1-9>"* and marked with a black "**X**" indicating the cache. Watches can occasionally be found here, suggesting they were placed there by the expedition members as markers of some kind.

They can be equipped by selecting them in the inventory, where they are named **"Old Photo <1-8> of 8"**. It is unknown why the handwriting on several of the photos does not match these numbers. One possible explanation is that multiple people wrote on the photos, hence the different handwriting.

## Cache Photo 1

Photo Location:  Photo 1 - Located in where Cave 3 and Cave 9 intersect. Near some old gun ammo.  
Cache Location:  Part 1 - A short distance south of the main cannibal village near the edge of the cliffs over looking the ocean.

## Cache Photo 2

Photo Location:  Photo 2 - Located at the end of Cave 9.  
Cache Location:  Part 2 - At the end of the large peninsula, behind the Sacred Tree, near the edge of the cliff over looking the ocean.

## Cache Photo 3

Photo Location:  Photo 3 - Inside Cave 5 (Submerged Cave). Enter the water room with Rusty Axe and swim to the end. Climb the rope, the photo is at top beside the Basic Fire.  
Cache Location:  Part 3 - Inside one of the huts of the Lakeside Village.

## Cache Photo 4

Photo Location:  Photo 4 - Inside Cave 7, next to last level at the bottom, beside the rock with the hole in it.  
Cache Location:  Part 4 - Near the Beach Hut Village, also close to a dead shark, near where the river comes out to the ocean.

## Cache Photo 5

Photo Location:  Photo 5 - Inside Cave 7, in the area behind the planks with the crosses. Turn around from the spot where Toy Arm 2 is to see a rope going down - climb down to the third ledge to find the photo (Note: the Restraining Order is on the second ledge).  
Cache Location:  Part 5 - Behind Geese Lake, a short distance from the edge of the cliff that goes down to Geese Lake.

## Cache Photo 6

Similar to Cache Photo 8, you cannot find Cache Photo 6 because of a bug that came to be in a patch and the developers never added its location back.  
Cache Location:  Part 6 - On the southernmost of the two islands near the shipping containers, be cautious swimming as there is a shark in those waters.

## Cache Photo 7

Photo Location:  Photo 7 - Located outside the entrance of Cave 3, just before you enter the cave. It is across the river from the riverside village and the fertile lands.  
Cache Location:  Part 7 - Near Snow Lake in the snow area.

## Cache Photo 8

Photo Location:  Photo 8 - Located just inside of the climbing entrance (the one near Center Village) to Cave 1, in a tent.  
Cache Location:  Part 8 - At the top left end of the map. Near the beach hut cannibal village but above it, near the cliff edges.

## Known Bugs

* Cache Photo 7 is labeled as "Dig Location #8".
* Cache Photo 8 is labeled as "Dig Location #9".
* Cache Photo 8 cannot be picked up, and as commands cannot place the photo in inventory, the root issue must be that there is no slot for it in the cache photo collection portion of inventory.
