**Herbal Medicines** is a craftable food item that was added in update v0.06 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Herbal Medicine will heal the player for 50 hit points when consumed, which is the same amount as the pill version. It also restores some fullness when consumed. It can added to a quick select slot to be used in emergencies. There is an improved version called Herbal Medicine+, it restores health to full. You can carry a max of 5 of each.
