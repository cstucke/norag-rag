**Infobox: Stick Bag**
**Description**
- **Added in:** v0.43
- **Item type:** Storage
- **Obtained through:** Crafted
- **Equipable:** No
- **Consumable:** No
- **Storage:** Cannot be stored
- **Other Effects:** Increase stick carrying capacity 10 to 20

The **stick bag** is a craftable inventory storage item which allows the player to carry up to ten extra sticks in their inventory. This is extremely helpful for building. This item also enables the player to build a temporary shelter anywhere, even in caves, so the player can save the game at any time. It operates the same as the throwable rock bag, quiver, and rock bag in that it permanently increases storage.
