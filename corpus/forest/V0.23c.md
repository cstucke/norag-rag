**v0.23c** was the second bugfix version for the v0.23 update. It was the second in a series of 3 bugfixes released on September 1, 2015. Like the subsequent update, it contained a single change note.

## Dev Notes

V0.23C Hotfix
Posted on 2 September, 2015 by Anna

Hey Everyone,

Here's another small issue we found with wall upgrades. When destroyed they could kick all clients from multiplayer game.

## Additions / Changes / Fixes

* Fixed bug where upgraded walls could kick players when destroyed
