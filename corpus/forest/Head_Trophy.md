The **head trophy**, also known as *Trophy Maker*, is a decorative building that can be placed on walls and then have a animal head attached to it. It was added in update v0.27 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Head trophy requires a stick to be built. The player can then add any animal head they wish to it. The following Animal Heads can be attached:  
 Boar Head  
 Crocodile Head  
 Deer Head  
 Goose Head  
 Lizard Head  
 Rabbit Head  
 Raccoon Head  
 Seagull Head  
 Shark Head  
 Squirrel Head  
 Tortoise Head  
 Mutant Baby Head  
 Virginia Head  
 Armsy Head  
 Cowman Head
