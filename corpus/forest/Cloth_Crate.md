**Cloth Crates** are game objects that contain 4 pieces of cloth.

## Gameplay

**Cloth Crates** can be found throughout the world. The player will be required to hit the crate with a weapon to open them. Enemies can knock these type of crates causing them to break open. These crates will respawn when you save and exit. They also respawn in caves when you exit and enter the cave again.

## Trivia

* **Cloth Crates** were in the game prior to v0.38b, however they weren't breakable. In v0.38b they were made breakable and to contain cloth.
* In patch notes for update v0.38b, they are referred to as *louis crates*. This is a reference to the modern company Louis Vuitton (note the pattern on the containers) and could not have been brought to the peninsula by the Christian Missionaries.

## Known bugs

If destroyed by an explosive, it will yield 8 Cloth instead of 4.
