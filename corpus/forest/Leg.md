**Legs** are meat items and body limbs that can chopped off dead cannibals or obtained from cannibal effigies in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Legs** can be chopped from cannibals corpses using an axe or any cutting melee weapon. They can also be found in certain cannibal effigies such as the "Skull Bag" effigy, even in peaceful mode. It can be used to build effigies, be used as a weak melee weapon and even cooked and eaten by the player.

As a weapon, the **Leg** only consumes a small amount of energy, which is regained between two swings, allowing the player to fight almost indefinitely. However, the **Leg** is very weak and a huge amount of hits are necessary to kill enemies, making the **Leg** a very last resort melee weapon, though it can still be used to block.

The **Leg** can be cooked on a fire and eaten by the player, as well as placed on a drying rack for later consumption. Leaving it to burn on a fire yields a single bone. As with any edible cannibal body limb, it will lower the player's sanity upon consumption, as well as raising weight like a snack would.

The player can only carry 2 **Legs** in his inventory.

## Trivia

* Body limbs are perishable, and will spoil if kept in the player's inventory for too long.
* Eating cooked body limbs is the fastest way to decrease sanity.
* Eating dried body limbs will fill your fullness bar just like regular dried meat would dependent on which state the body limb is in.
* Eating dried body limb does not decrease sanity
