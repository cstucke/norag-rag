The **Charles: The Survival Man** book is a decorative item that cannot be collected or interacted with, it was added in update v0.01 in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Charles: The Survival Man**, also referred to as the **movie** or **TV script,** is a non-interactive story item that can be found in the largest abandoned camp, the Film Crew Camp. It is speculated that this **script** was for a movie that was going to be recorded on the Peninsula. The **script** could be an easter egg, or it may contribute to the story in the future. It was written in June 1984 by **Charles Lai**, who perhaps was there himself to film the **script**.

## Trivia

* This item suggests that the film crew could be somehow tied to the yacht inhabitants.
* The bodies in the **film crew camp** are much too fresh to be from 1984, the date listed on ***Charles***. They still have flies buzzing around. These could possible be unaccounted passengers that were killed by the cannibals, as cannibals frequently patrol the area.
* The Yacht Magazine also has the year 1984 on it (along with being in summer, like ***Charles*** at June 2), which seems too unlikely to be a complete coincidence.
* The dead Yacht inhabitants that the player can see (one on board, one in cave 2) are old enough to match the year for ***Charles,*** being mummified like they should given their age.
* The tents in the **film crew camp** are noticeably older than the other modern tents that can be found. They have rips and tears in the roofs, unlike the others, again suggesting they're from 1984. Items could’ve been taken from the camp down to Cave 2, as a small section of the cave holds camping equipment and a Cassette Player.
* The name **Charles Lai** (the author of the **Charles: The Survival Man** script) can be seen in the endgame credits in the **SPECIAL THANKS** section. Both **Charles Lai** and Endnight Games director **Ben Falcone** worked on the movie Tron: Legacy from 2010, with **Ben Falcone** as a **lighting artist** and **Charles Lai** as a **2D compositing artist**.
