**Yellow crates** are game objects that can be opened for flares or modern arrows, they were added in update v0.26 of *The Forest*

## Gameplay

**Yellow crates** are similar to suitcases in that they need to struck to be opened. Aim at the ground with a weapon and strike the crate. Once it is open it will reveal its contents. It appears that the game has set the contents of **yellow crates**, they will either have flares or modern arrows in them.
