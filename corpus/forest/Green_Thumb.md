## Description

*Grow all plant types*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

To complete this achievement, the player will need to build a small garden and acquire the seeds from gathering various plants on the peninsula. **Seeds** can then be planted in the garden.

### Requirements

Aloe Vera seed  
  Coneflower seed  
  Blueberry seed  
 seed planted in the Garden
