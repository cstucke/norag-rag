# Small plants
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Small_plants
Categories: Item, Flora
Revision: 65746

There are four varieties of small flowering plants that are listed in the Nature Guide section of the Survival Guide. These plants sometimes replace herb spawns, so you may find herbs if you re-visit the same spot later. When destroyed with a weapon, they produce 1-2 leaves. Unlike some herbs, they never drop seeds. Despite their small size, they also provide a camouflage bonus for the stealthy player.

## Dandelion

*taraxacum officinale*

## Fireweed

*Chamerion angustifolium*

## Horsetail

*Equisetum*

## Thistle

*Carduus marianus*
