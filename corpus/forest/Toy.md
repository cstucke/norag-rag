**Broken Toys** are pieces of a robot toy that Timmy was holding before the crash which were added in update v0.12 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The pieces can be placed on the crafting mat to "repair" the **toy**. The whole **toy** can be used as a weapon. It has a slow swing speed, though it does not use energy. It's damage is equivalent to that of the modern axe.

## Achievement

Once you complete the game with the toy fully assembled, this will complete the achievement Good Father.

### Console Command Method

Using Console Commands, this achievement can be obtained by teleporting to the parts, adding them to the inventory, or by spawning them. Here are the commands:

| Name | Add Item | Spawn Item | Remove Item | Teleport |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Broken Toy Head** | `additem 133` | `spawnitem 133` | `removeitem 133` | `goto -440 22 952` |
| **Broken Toy Arms** | `additem 134` | `spawnitem 134` | `removeitem 134` | `goto -791 32 -2` `goto 263 25 -836` |
| **Broken Toy Legs** | `additem 135` | `spawnitem 135` | `removeitem 135` | `goto 841 17 881` `goto 42 -289 -273` |
| **Broken Toy Body** | `additem 136` | `spawnitem 136` | `removeitem 136` | `goto -433 -17 594` |

## Bugs

* The **Toy** is heavily bugged. When saving and returning, the game may remove arms or legs from your toy if you have two of the same on it.
* The **Toy** appears to be bugged with the "Good Father" achievement, it may not be achievable in multiplayer
