The **Coaster Cave** or Cave 13 is a small submerged cave off the southeast coast of the Peninsula, near three small islands.

It has one swim entrance, which requires ~50% of a full air canister in the rebreather to get in and out of the cave. The entrance is also marked with a floating piece of wood, similarly to other submerged caves. Once inside the entrance, there is a deep cylindrical room which leads to a middle room serving as an air pocket, with another deep cylindrical space going to the final room with the Log Track schematic on a podium, with a wooden model behind it. Like the other small caves added in v1.10, the **Coaster Cave** contains no enemies.
