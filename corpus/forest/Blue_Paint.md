**Blue Paint** was a crafting material item that was added in v0.12 and removed in v1.0 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Blue paint** was used to make wood targets, it was also used to paint the Plane Axe, Upgraded Stick, and the Upgraded Rock blue. Painting these weapons was purely cosmetic. The developers did not state a reason for removing **blue paint** or orange paint from the game. The resulting item would be a blue-colored version of the original with all previously applied weapon upgrades still on it. Orange paint wouldn't remain on the item after painting it with **blue paint**. The effect wouldn't wear off when under rain or swimming.
