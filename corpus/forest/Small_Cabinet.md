The **Small Cabinet**, also known as the *Medicine Cabinet*, is a storage structure that was added in update v0.13 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **small cabinet**, much like its bigger cousin, the large cabinet, can be found on the second page of the storage section in the survival guide.

It provides space for up to 9 items of the ones named above. When facing the **small cabinet**, the item to be placed can be cycled through using the *Rotate* key (default key `R`).
As it takes up as much space as the large cabinet while providing two storage slots less, the player should consider building large cabinets instead.

After being placed, the **small cabinet** can be destroyed by hitting it repeatedly with a weapon of choice. As with other objects, it neither drops any resources nor its contents upon destruction.
