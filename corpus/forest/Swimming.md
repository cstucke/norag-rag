**Swimming** is the action of travelling through the water. To swim, the player must move using the directional keys as if they were walking while in the water. The player can't use any items while swimming, though they can jump out of the water if there is an object to jump onto. When swimming, weapons and other selected items are returned to the inventory. The player is the only creature capable of swimming; all others just run underwater.

When underwater, the player has a breath meter which slowly drains; when equipped with the rebreather and a full tank of air, the player can stay underwater for 300 seconds.

## Bugs

* Often, when the player re-emerges from the water, he will have the Plane Axe equipped, regardless of whatever was equipped before.
