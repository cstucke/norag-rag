The **Yacht Photo,** or **the photo of Yacht,** is a story item in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **yacht photo** can be found at beachside grave, right near the yacht. Its marked with a cross and some rubble over the grave. There is also Cassette 5 next to the photo. Previously, it was possible to find it at the mountain grave.

## Theory

On the **yacht photo** is written *June '82*. This could indicate when the Yacht arrived to the Peninsula. Alternatively, this could show the Yacht off the shore of the Peninsula before it was stolen. More information can be found on the yacht page.
