Here are some more fixes for last night's update:

## Additions, Changes, & Fixes

* Fixed area where you could fall through ocean at edge of world
* Fixed left over underwater cave entrance that lead to nowhere
* Fixed an issue where microstutter would occur if game was started from a new game (issue didn't affect saves)
* Added possible fix for radeon 5 and 6 series cards that were having issues launching game
