The **rock wall** is a custom building and a defensive building made of only rocks, they are an extremely versatile in what they can do.

## Gameplay

**Rock walls** can be built by selecting a starting point and "pulling" until it has reached the desired length. The player can also stack multiple **rock walls** on top of each other to make a higher one. A cannibal can jump over a wall that is only one or two layers high.

They can be built into single pillars, platforms, walls, houses and many other things.

**Rock walls** can be used as a defensive wall and can be a great alternative to the defensive wall. They must be built three layers high to prevent cannibals from jumping over them.

If the player is building in an area where logs are limited, they can use **rock walls** instead, this can make the building process easier as rocks spawn almost everywhere and very often.

Building **rock walls** can take a very long time. The player can do the following to speed up the process:

* Craft the rock bag to carry additional rocks (10 instead of 5)
