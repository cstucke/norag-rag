The **cross** is a tall totem like building that was added in update V1.10 to *The Forest*. It can be unlocked by finding the schematics in the Shipwreck Cave.

### Gameplay

It serves no purpose other than decoration. It requires 48 sticks, 34 logs, and 18 rocks.
