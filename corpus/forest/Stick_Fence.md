**Stick Fences** are custom buildings that were added in update v0.23 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

It can be found under the Custom Building section of the Survival Guide. **Stick fences** serve as a defensive wall against enemies but can be destroyed. The fences can be placed like the defensive wall or Rock Wall and can also be autofilled on Custom Foundations , Custom Floors and Custom Roofs . It is half as strong, but easier to make as the Bone Fence. Enemies can jump over one layer of fences but if you stack two or three layers, they won't be able to.
