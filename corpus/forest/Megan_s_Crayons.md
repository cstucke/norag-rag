**Megan's Crayons,** or the **crayon box,** is a story item that was added in update v0.51 to *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

**Megan's crayons** is similar to the crucifix and paintbrush in that the player can interact with it and will examine it closely, but it will not be added to the notes section of the survival guide. The **crayon box** has "Property of Megan" prominently featured on the back.

## Theory

It is assumed that these are the same crayons used to create Megan Drawings, along with being the same crayons used to murder Dr. Cross. The **crayon box** can only be found in the research lab.
