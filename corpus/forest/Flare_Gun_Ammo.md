**Flare gun ammo** is craftable ammunition used exclusively for the Flare Gun. It was added in update v0.01 and was made craftable in update v0.21 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Flare gun ammo** is crafted by combining flares with the flare gun. It does not consume the flare gun in the process. **Flare gun ammo** is not found in the inventory; to see how much ammo you have, place the cursor over the flare gun and it will indicate a number. This number indicates how much ammo you have. You can hold a maximum of 10 flares though you can hold 10,000 **flare gun ammo**, so It is recommended to make **flare gun ammo** when you are at max flare capacity to ensure there is no wastage. As the flare gun re-spawns when you save and exit, collecting it again will add 5 more **flare gun ammo**

## Locations

**Flare Gun Ammo** cannot be found, it must be crafted using flares. Here are locations of Yellow Crates that contain flares you can use to make **flare gun ammo**:  
 - near the Cockpit  
 - at the Film Crew Camp  
 - Main Cannibal Village  
 - Hut near **Main Cannibal Village**  
 - Modern Bow location  
 - Cave 6 - Lawyer Cave

## Notes

* When fired from the Flare Gun, a flare will burn for 30 seconds, during which time it will emit light and a plume of coloured smoke.
* Once you have stacked 10,000 **flare gun ammo** you will still be able to craft the item however it is hit and miss, quite often requiring all 5 flares to be crafted in order to add 1 more flare gun ammo to your inventory.
