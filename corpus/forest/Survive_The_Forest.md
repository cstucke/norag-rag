# Survive The Forest
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Survive_The_Forest
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74716

## Description

*Finish Game*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

The **button** that needs to be pressed to get the achievement is located in the **Observatory** of the Sahara Laboratory. To access the **Lab**, the player must go through the Vault Door, located at the end of Cave HC at the bottom of the sinkhole.

After taking down the plane the screen cuts to black with the text "One Year Later" and Doran Show and **Adult Timmy** cutscenes start. After both cutscenes, the End Game Credits roll after which the player gets the achievement.

### Requirements

Rebreather to access the location  
 Climbing Axe to access the location  
  Keycard 1 and Keycard 2 to access the location  
 choosing normal ending
