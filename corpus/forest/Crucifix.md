The **Crucifix** is an examined item added in v0.13 and found deep within the caves. Picking it up uses the 'examine' animation, and has the new examine icon that is shaped like an eye. It doesn't give any advantage to the player as enemies do not react to it. It's an assumption that it could be part of the story in the future.

The **Crucifix** can be found in Cave 1 by the **wooden barrier**.
