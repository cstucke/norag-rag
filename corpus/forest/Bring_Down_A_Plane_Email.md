The **Bring Down A Plane Email** is a story item in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Deep in the Sahara Research Lab, the player can find the **Bring Down A Plane Email**, left in a printer near the body of Dr. Cross. Upon discovery, the player will examine the item and then it will be added to the notes section of the survival guide. The email shows correspondence between Dr. Cross and Sasha Bondi. It appears that someone has set a coffee or other drink on the document, staining it and making parts hard to read. This document suggests the existence of the Power Obelisk before the player finds it later on. It heavily suggests that Dr. Cross, because he knew of the power of the two obelisks, used the obelisk to bring down the plane that Timmy and the player were on. Nearby the email, the player can find another, non-interactable email that reiterates what the first says.
