The **mountain area grave** was found east of the lake in the mountain area. It was removed from the game, most likely in v1.0.

The fifth  Cassette Tape  
 and several pictures of the yacht could be found on top of it. The picture leaning on the cross was added to the notes when picked up. There's also a secret cache located in the immediate vicinity of the grave's location.

Who exactly is buried here is unknown, but the pictures indicate that it was a member of the yacht crew.
