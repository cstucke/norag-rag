**Chainsaw advertisement** is a story item that was added in update v0.60 in *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

**Chainsaw advertisement** was added to the game with the Chainsaw. It doesn't appear to have much involvement in the story, though it could have been used by the cavers to clear out trees on the surface. The chain in the advertisement is the exact same chainsaw that can be found in the game. It is possible that the advertisement is to indicate to players that there is a chainsaw in the game, if they haven't found it already.

## Location

The **chainsaw advertisement** can be found on the ground right next to the Climbing Axe. It is surrounded by about 4 mutant babies and a few pale cannibals.
