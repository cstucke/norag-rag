The **Cockpit** is the missing front of the crashed plane in which you spawn. It was added in update v0.18 to ***The Forest***. It was moved in update v1.0 to the mountain area.

## Gameplay

Inside the old Cockpit you could see one of the dead pilots laying over the controls. Located near the cockpit was the co-pilot sitting in a chair and there is a flare gun behind him on a seat. In the new version the copilot is in the cockpit and the pilot was thrown through the front window.
