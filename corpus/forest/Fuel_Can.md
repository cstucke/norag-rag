**Fuel** is a material item that is used to power the chainsaw, it was added alongside it in update v0.60 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

Aside from powering the Chainsaw, **Fuel** currently has no other uses.

## Locations

There are 22 cans of **fuel** in the game and most locations contain two cans of **fuel**.
Here is a list of '**The Forest Map'** locations:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| - Center Village  - Near Main Village   - Main Village   - Main Village   - Waterfall Village   - Beach Hut Village   - Snow Camp | - Cave 5  - Cave 6   - Cave 8   - Cave 7 near Pedometer   - Cave 3 near Chainsaw   - Cave 3 NOT near Chainsaw   - Cave 1 near Katana |

## Notes

* the chainsaw running at full throttle (attack button held down) will consume one Fuel Can every 80 seconds.
* the chainsaw does not consume fuel whilst idling.
* **fuel cans** on the surface do not respawn, while most of the **fuel cans** in caves respawn both on single-player and multi-player.
