# Cave Explorers
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Cave_Explorers
Categories: Story
Revision: 74863

The **Cave Explorers**, also known as **modern cave explorers,** are groups of spelunkers who climbed, explored and studied caves all over the Forest’s peninsula. Their trace can be seen in **all** caves and cave entrances around the map, including Cave Science - Sahara Cave and Artifact Cave.

Their equipment like the **Climbing Axe**, **climbing ropes**, **orange tents**, **remotely operated underwater vehicle (ROV)**, **caving helmets,** and others are modern and unique so it's fairly easy to see what parts of the map were they exploring in particular.

## Items

* Climbing Axe
* Compass
* Map
* Flares and Flare Crates
* Snacks
* Modern Bow and Modern Arrows
* Chainsaw and Fuel Cans

## Props

* Orange Tents
* Sleeping Bags
* Remote Operated Underwater Vehicles (ROV)
* Cave climbing ropes
* Caving helmet
* Marshmallows
* Pots and pans
* Empty soda cans
* Laptops
* Waterproof cardboard boxes (found in camp with Modern Bow)

The ROVs emit light and can be pushed around but cannot be interacted with. This suggests that they were used as makeshift lights, being controlled from the laptops found at certain camps.

## Theory

* Among the other caves, **Cave Explorers** were exploring Cave Science - Sahara Cave that is probably only accessible through the Sahara Laboratory which means that they were the employees of Sahara Therapeutics or hired by them. Evidence of this can be seen with one camp in Cave 6 - Lawyer Cave, which has an open briefcase with multiple redacted documents. This second wave of explorers seem to have died due to a mix of cannibalism and natural causes like collapsing rocks or starvation.
* If the latter is the case, it should be noted that caving equipment can be found in Sahara Cave and Artifact Cave, which suggests they helped map out caves that were used to store items or become part of the facility.
* They shouldn't be confused with "older" cave explorers (possibly Christian Missionaries) with white tents as their equipment and tools are primitive. Also, the "older" cave explorers have story items like Beneath The Limestone and (the now removed) Practical Caver dating from *June 5th 1932*.
