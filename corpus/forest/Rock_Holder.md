The **rock holder** is a storage structure that holds rocks and has two variants. The small rock holder can hold 20 rocks and the large rock holder can hold 40 rocks.

## Gameplay

To store rocks approach the rock holder and press `C`. To withdraw rocks approach the rock holder and press `E`.

The rock holder blueprint can be located on the storage page in the survival guide.

To construct the **large** rock holder place the default rock holder blueprint, approach the blueprint ghost, and press `R`. Repeating these actions will revert the rock holder to the default blueprint.
