The **Waterfall Village** is a mutant built location.

## Gameplay

The **waterfall village** is a mutant village that is often used by mutants as an exchange for their patrols. It's one of the most valuable villages for raiding as it has a lot of different **items** and reach **flora** and **fauna**.
