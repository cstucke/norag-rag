The **Explosive Wire Trap** is a one use only trap that was added in update v0.14 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The explosion is powerful enough to destroy several layers of walls, so the **trap** shouldn't be placed close to walls and buildings. It is not possible to destroy, deconstruct or otherwise remove the **trap**. The player can trigger this by running through it, so it is recommended to keep a safe distance after construction. It can be triggered by certain projectiles, all arrows and flintlock pistol shots.
