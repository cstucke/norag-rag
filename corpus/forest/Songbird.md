# Songbird
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Songbird
Categories: Creature, Animal
Revision: 40791

The Songbird is a common animal found perching on various items or flying around at early morning or on the beach. If the player stands around for long enough, the player's hand may come up and allow a song bird to perch on it (just like the 2nd trailer showed). The songbird can be killed for feathers, although they will most likely fly away on approach.
