**Masked Skinny cannibals**, also known as *Masked Starving Cannibals*, are the strongest skinny type cannibal in the game. They were added in update v0.55 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Masked skinny cannibals** are similar to masked cannibals. Although they are much smaller, they almost always travel together despite having very different stats and behaviors.
**Masked skinny cannibals** are almost exactly the same as skinny cannibals and pale skinny cannibals, however, they are the strongest of the three skinny cannibal types.

Once they first appear, they slowly begin to replace all pale skinny cannibal patrols that appear above ground, with the exception of the pale scouts travelling with creepy mutants. While masked skinny cannibals are faster than the larger masked cannibals, they suffer when it comes to raw power.

## Trivia

* Like the Masked Cannibal, Pale Skinny Cannibal and Pale Cannibal, there is no female version of this mutant in the game.
