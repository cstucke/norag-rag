The **Main Cannibal Village** is the main cannibal built location.

## Gameplay

The **Main Cannibal Village** is by far the largest **village** that the **cannibals** have built. It is often patrolled by **cannibals** and is a dangerous location to build or loiter in. It is one of the best **villages** to raid, as it has a large amount of cloth, other valuable items and Cave 2 - Hanging Cave climbing entrance.
