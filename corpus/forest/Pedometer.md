The **pedometer** is a tool item that was added in update v0.03 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

When held, the **pedometer** will display your current number of steps. It doesn't need to be held permanently since it is counting your steps even while in your inventory.

## Location

The **Pedometer** can be found deep inside the Cave 7 - Chasm Cave. The entrance is located here on the Island. You can find it in the room just before the Tennis Racket, on a tennis player corpse. The room is guarded by a Virginia and several cannibals.

## Trivia

* On previous versions of the game, the **Pedometer** could be found directly inside the plane.
* The **pedometer** used to look like a mobile phone, it also showed the temperature. However this was removed in v0.21.
