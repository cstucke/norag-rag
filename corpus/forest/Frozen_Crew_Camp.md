The **frozen crew camp** is a camping location with 3 frozen bodies located far north in the mountains, at the edge of the map.

## Gameplay

It is unknown why they froze to death because they seem to be dressed well for cold temperatures and they owned a durable tent. They froze to death with **snacks** and **sodas** in their hands. The **frozen crew camp** can be a good place to visit in the snow area because it has valuable items, sleeping and a saving spot.
