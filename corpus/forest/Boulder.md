# Boulder
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Boulder
Categories: Decorative Object
Revision: 74536

**Boulders** are decorative objects that cannot be picked up. They're mostly much larger than rocks and small rocks.

## Gameplay

Climbing a nearby boulder can help a lot with spotting smaller animals and getting a clean shot in with ranged weapons.

The player should avoid having boulders too close to the outside of their defensive walls, because they sometimes allow cannibals to jump over the walls.
