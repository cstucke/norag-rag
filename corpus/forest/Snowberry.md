**Snowberries** are harvestable white poisonous berries and material food items that can be found in ***The Forest***. For most of the game's history they had no crafting abilities, however in update v0.72, they were made into a material for crafting which operate exactly like twinberries.

## Gameplay

**Snowberries** can be found on snowberries bushes. They are poisonous and will damage the players health when consumed, they do not provide any hydration and they cannot be placed in stews. On normal and peaceful mode, this damage is only 1 health point. Though on hard mode and especially hard survival mode, this damage can be lethal. **Snowberry** bushes look the same as blueberry bushes though they have white colored berries instead of blue colored berries. The type of berries are randomly assigned and every game has a different type of berry attached. They will often change berry types when the player saves and exits. Harvesting **snowberry** bushes does not provide any seeds and you can only obtain 3 berries from each bush. With a pouch, you can collect **snowberries** from snowberry bushes, but you can only carry 30 **snowberries** at a time.

## Warning

Do not consume twinberries or snowberries when playing hard survival mode. They will damage the player 30 health points per berry consumed. At 100 health, 4 berries will kill the player.
