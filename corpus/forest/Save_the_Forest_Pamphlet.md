The **Save the Forest Pamphlet** is a decorative item that cannot be collected or interacted with, it was added in v0.42 in ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **Save the Forest Pamphlet** can be found in the blue tent on the beach near the anchor and sharks. The textures are poor quality and difficult to make out. There are most likely other copies around the game.
