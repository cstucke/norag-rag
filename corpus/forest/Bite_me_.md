# Bite me!
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Bite_me!
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74740

## Description

*Kill shark*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

Sharks spawn both in single player and multiplayer. The quickest way to complete this achievement is to start a normal game and make your way to the yacht on the south side of the Peninsula. A shark typically spawns just south to southeast of the yacht. Building a small raft is recommended, considering kiting sharks to the shore will be difficult and can kill players in two hits if one chooses to swim in the ocean. Armor does not effect this.

It is easier to see the sharks through the water surface if users decide to change ocean quality to "Flat", which is located in Options > Graphics. According to some, users believe sharks will only spawn when the player is swimming nearby, but this has not been confirmed. Once you find a shark, they can be killed using arrows or spears.

### Requirements

killing a Shark
