The **Wood Target** is a utility structure that was added in update v0.15 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **wood target** is a building intended for practicing your ranged weapon and throw skills, and it can be found the utility section of the Survival Guide. The **wood target** is useful for practicing your aiming with the bow and arrows can be collected from the surface of the target after it has been used.

The **Wood Target** is unique in that it doesn't take player weapon damage, with the exception being any of the melee weapons.

The **wood target** can be used as a wall when placed in reverse, it can block mutants out completely, however cannibals can jump over it. Mutants can still glitch over the top if the surface it is placed on is elevated.
