**v0.23d** was the third bugfix version for the v0.23 update. It was the third in a series of 3 bugfixes released on September 1, 2015. Like the previous update, it contained a single change note.

## Dev Notes

V0.23D Hotfix
Posted on 2 September, 2015 by Anna

Hey everyone,

3 hotfixes this morning! This one fixes an issue with holding a fire arrow for too long (over 30 seconds) and then firing it causes clients to be kicked from multiplayer games
