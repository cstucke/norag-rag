**Orange Paint** was a crafting material item that was added in v0.12 and removed in v1.0 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**Orange paint** was used to paint the Plane Axe, Upgraded Stick, and the Upgraded Rock orange. Painting these weapons was purely cosmetic. The developers did not state a reason for removing **orange paint** or blue paint from the game. The resulting item would be an orange-colored version of the original with all previously applied weapon upgrades still on it. Blue paint wouldn't remain on the item after painting it with **orange paint**. The effect wouldn't wear off when under rain or swimming.
