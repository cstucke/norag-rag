**Rope** is a craftable material item used in many buildings and items, it was added in v0.03 of ***The Forest***. Furthermore, it was made craftable in update v0.18.

## Gameplay

**Ropes** are found hanging in caves, in cannibal villages, on the Yacht, as well as on small boats near lakes and on the beach. The player inventory can hold a maximum of 4. When exiting a cave, the ropes inside will respawn when re-entering.

There are visually two versions of ropes: cream-colored rough ones, and professional climbing green ones. They will always appear like the first version in the inventory or in certain buildings, but green on others.

There is no official storage for **ropes**, though by placing a blueprint of a ceiling skull lamp, you can place one **rope** inside, and then cancel it when needed. It has a 100% chance of returning the **rope**. These can be placed in house boats as well. (Note: Every time you leave and rejoin the rope at the boat will respawn.)
