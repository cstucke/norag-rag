**Keycard Two** is a collectible story item required to advance the plot of The Forest.

Although both advance the story, the gold **Keycard Two** should not be confused with the employee Keycard.

## Gameplay

The gold colored **Keycard Two** opens security doors within the Sahara Laboratory. It does not open the Vault Door entryway to the lab. From the Obelisk room, **Keycard Two** allows access through the corridor marked "This Way to Second Artifact" leading to Cave Sahara. However, **Keycard Two** is not required to operate the elevator up to the Observatory. It can be found next to the Resurrection Obelisk only after defeating the End Boss. It is located on the inventory screen backpack next to the first Keycard. It appears much cleaner than the other keycards.

Additionally, the gold colored **Keycard Two** may be used to unlock the security door on the Yacht. Depending on choices made by the player near the conclusion of the story, **Keycard Two** may be used to access an artifact on the Yacht resembling a toy spaceship, return to the end game cave, place the artifact on the pedestal, and open the door leading to the Artifact Ball. The door to the Artifact Ball is unique from the other pressure activated doors in caves. This door requires a unique item rather than rocks to make the pressure plate work.
