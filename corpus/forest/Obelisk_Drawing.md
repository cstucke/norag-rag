**Obelisk Drawing** is a story item found on an office table of the research lab and not to be confused with the various background props found scattered around the peninsula.

## Gameplay

They depict the resurrection obelisk, which can be used to bring the dead to life, as the name suggests. The drawings take many forms. It is suggested by the multitudinous nature of these drawings that the obelisk plays a key role in many a visitor's decision to come to the peninsula.

The **Obelisk Drawings** are not to be confused with the Obelisk Photo, which is an interactive and collectible item. Neither are they to be confused with the Obelisk Sketch, which is a collectible version of an **Obelisk Drawing**.

## Location

* Cave 5, near the sacrifice door
* the table in the Yacht
* scattered around the research lab

## Theory

The presence of the drawings on the yacht, in conjunction with the Virginia photo, indicates that the yacht group was aware of the contents and dangers of the Peninsula before arriving.
