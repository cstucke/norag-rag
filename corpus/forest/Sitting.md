# Sitting
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Sitting
Categories: Pages with image sizes containing extra px, Gameplay
Revision: 54226

**Sitting** refers to the buildings the player can build and sit on that can restore energy and sanity. They all have the same effect, regardless of their cost. Benches are the cheapest to build, making them the most viable.
