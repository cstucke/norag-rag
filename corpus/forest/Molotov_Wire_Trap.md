The **Molotov Wire Trap** is a single-use trap that was added in update v0.28 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

It instantly creates a molotov cocktail flame when triggered. The flame lingers for as long as a normal cocktail flame, and will harm both players and enemies. It can be triggered by certain projectiles, all arrows and flintlock pistol shots.
Placing a **molotov wire trap** on top of a leaf pile trap can add extra burning length, as the **molotov wire trap** will trigger the **leaf trap**.
