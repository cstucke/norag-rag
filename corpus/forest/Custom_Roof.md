**Custom roofs** are custom buildings that provide shelter from rain and snow when built, most of the time. They were added in update v0.14 of *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

**Custom roofs** are the only roof you can build that provide protection from rain and snow, most of the time. Currently, custom floors do not offer protection from rain. **Custom roofs** can be attached to many types of structures. They can serve many purposes, not just roofs. They can be used as ramps, scaffolding, bridges etc. Like most other custom buildings, **custom roofs** have a manual mode by pressing `C` while placing it. This can allow the player much more freedom when placing roofs or building unique designs.
**Custom roofs** are extremely expensive and can often cost more than the custom foundations or custom floors. Ensure you are counting the logs when placing a roof. The higher you go, more logs will start to appear in the roof. If your building is wide, this could convert to 4 logs across instead of 2 logs across. 4 logs across is very expensive and should be avoided.

## Known Bugs

* When moving blueprints of Custom Roof over/into existing Defensive Walls, the blueprint will freeze in mid-air, and you have to restart usually.
* Rain and snow sometimes come through the custom roof. It is unknown why this happens. Apparently this is a long-standing bug within the game.
