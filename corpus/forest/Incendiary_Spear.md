**Incendiary Spear** is a crafted extension of the Upgraded Spear it was added in update v0.34 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

It can be crafted and can be thrown for a one time use. It will inflict burning damage when thrown at enemies. It doesn't need to be lit with the lighter, similar to the molotov wire trap. A weak spear cannot receive the **Incendiary Spear** upgrade.

Whilst an upgraded spear is an **Incendiary Spear**, it can still be used for normal melee attacks with out issue. It doesn't change the stats at all.

The upgraded spear isn't destroyed when the **Incendiary Spear** explodes, it can be collected afterwards.
