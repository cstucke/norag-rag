**Sam Sounde** is an author whose name is mentioned on several books within ***The Forest***.

## Story

**Sam Sounde** co authored the Survival Guide, along with Eric LeBlanc. Both their names can be seen on the cover of the book. **Sam Sounde** also appears to be the author of The Dark Haired Man Book and Sam Sounde SKS Book. The Dark Haired Man Book is a book you can collect, the player will place a piece of it inside the survival guide. Sam Sounde SKS Book cannot be collected, it is currently only a decoration that can be found within the game.

Sam Sounde would continue to author books after the events of The Forest, even founding his own publishing company. Some of these books can be found on the SOTF wiki.
