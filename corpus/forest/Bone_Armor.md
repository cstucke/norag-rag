The **Bone Armor** is a type of armor added by v0.33 update of *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

The **Bone Armor** provides much more protection than lizard armor. It does have the side effect of being louder when worn so won't be as effective for stealth. Each piece worn will increase the player's armor by 40, and wearing full **Bone Armor** (10 pieces) will give the player a total armor of 400.

The player can carry up to 10 pieces of **Bone Armor** in their inventory.

## Trivia

* It has been speculated that wearing **Bone Armor** would increase cannibal hostility towards the player, likely due to its intimidating appearance.
* The head piece of bone armor is shaped like a skull, but it will still require six bones and three cloths instead of a skull. The jaw bone is attached to the torso section however.
* Despite being made partially of cloth, it doesn't protect the player from the cold or cold damage.

## Known bugs

* If you wear **bone armor** while your armor is full, it may disappear.
* Switching armors only works sometimes, at others it can make the armor disappear.
