**Explosives Crates** are game objects that contain 4-5 sticks of dynamite.

## Gameplay

**Explosives Crates** can be found throughout the world. There can be opened crates that have dynamite in them, such crates can be found in the Main Village and in some caves. **Explosives crates** require the player to hit the crate with a weapon to open them. Enemies can knock these type of crates causing them to break open. These crates will respawn when you save and exit. They also respawn in caves when you exit and enter the cave again.
