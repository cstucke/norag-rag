**Plane meals** or **food trays** are a food item that cannot be collected; they can only be eaten on the spot. From the model, the meal appears to contain slices of meat with rice and vegetables, plus an additional food item--possibly a wrapped dessert or condiment, or possibly a filet of a fish, like salmon.

## Gameplay

**Plane meals** are found in the plane, where you spawn. They cannot be collected, moved, or stored. They will respawn every time the player saves and exits. They are almost essential where starting the game as the player starts with 0 hunger.

## Locations

* The Plane - 4x meals on the plane
* Cave 1 - Where the Katana is located
* Sahara Laboratory
