**Booze** is a consumable and material item that was added during alpha release of *The Forest*.

## Gameplay

Booze can be found in many places across the game world. The plane wreck contains a few bottles which the player can use, and it is also found in large quantities along the beach, suitcases and caves. Booze satisfies thirst at the cost of energy, and can be used to create a variety of different items, such as the Molotov and bombs. It can be crafted with a melee weapon using tree sap, increasing damage by adding broken glass. Inventory can hold a maximum of 7. Using booze will temporarily reset all cold effects.

## Trivia

One of the inventory bottles is "Jeff Daniels Whiskey" - referencing "Jack Daniels Whiskey".
Despite Booze being used to craft a variety of fire-related items in the game, no alcoholic beverage in real life actually contains alcohol in high enough concentrations to be flammable enough for these applications. The crafting behaviour of Booze is more akin to that of nearly pure alcohol solutions such as rubbing alcohol.
