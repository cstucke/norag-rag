The **Pouch** is a tool item made of rabbit fur which was added in v0.12 to ***The Forest***. The **pouch** is used to collect certain berries & mushrooms which are then stored in the player's inventory.

## Gameplay

When the player has a pouch equipped and approaches a bush of berries or mushroom, the "eat" icon over the berries will turn into a "collect" one. By pressing 'e', the berries will be stored in the inventory instead of eaten. This, along with a farm, allows for a passive, renewable food resource that can be grown at one's base.

Only one pouch can be held in the inventory at once, but they do not actually hold the berries and mushrooms, but rather add them to the player's inventory.

You do not require a pouch receive berries or mushrooms in multiplayer. They can be shared via the metal tin tray.

The **pouch** can collect all berries & mushrooms:  
 Blackberries  
 Blueberries  
 Snowberries  
 Twinberries  
 Amanita Mushroom  
 Chanterelle Mushroom  
 Deer Mushroom  
 Jack Mushroom  
 Liberty Cap Mushroom  
 Puff Mushroom
