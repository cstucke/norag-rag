Jessica Cross is the mother of Megan Cross and ex-partner of Dr. Mathew Cross, aka The Red Man. The family lived in Pringle Township, British Columbia, Canada. However, Mathew proved to be an abusive and unstable man, to the point Jessica had to file a restraining order against him. She later was murdered in a homicide by multiple stab wounds and head trauma. Given Dr. Cross's unhinged nature, it is implied that Mathew killed Jessica himself in a fit of rage and took custody of Megan by force.
