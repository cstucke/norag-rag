The **Weapon Rack** is a weapon storage building that was added in update v0.13 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **weapon rack** allows players to store most Weapons outside of their inventory. It's also a good way to share weapons with other players. To place an item on the rack you need to equip it, walk up to the rack and press `C`. To take a weapon from the rack you press `E` on whatever weapon you want to take.

## Storage

The **weapon rack** can store 4 of the following weapons/tools:  
 Chainsaw  
 Climbing Axe  
 Club  
 Crafted Axe  
 Crafted Bow  
 Crafted Club  
 Crossbow  
 Flare Gun  
 Flintlock Pistol  
 Incendiary Spear  
 Katana  
 Machete  
 Modern Axe  
 Modern Bow  
 Rusty Axe  
 Slingshot  
 Tennis Racket  
 Upgraded Spear  
 Weak Spear  
  
It **CANNOT** store the following weapons/tools:  
 Plane Axe  
 Repair Tool  
 Turtle Shell  
 Upgraded Rock  
 Upgraded Stick  
 Hairspray
