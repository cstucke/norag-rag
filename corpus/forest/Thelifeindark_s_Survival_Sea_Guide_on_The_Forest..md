## **Getting Started**

When you begin the game the first thing you must do after looting everything you find near the plane crash is to look for the sea. Once you find it set up a small temporary tent,(it is also recommended that you build a log sled) and after doing so put down the prints to a house boat and start building it. After you are done you should build 2 water collectors as indicated in the image on the right.
