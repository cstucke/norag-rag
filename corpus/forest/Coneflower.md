**Coneflower** is an harvestable plant, craftable and consumable item that was added in update v0.01 to ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

The **coneflower** is a tall, cone-shaped flower, recognizable by its white petals with an orange center. It can be used to craft energy mix, energy mix +, and herbal medicine+. It can be eaten to restore a small amount of health. When gathered, the player has a small chance to collect coneflower seeds, which can be planted in a small garden or a wall planter.

The player can only carry 10 coneflower in their inventory at a time.

## Growing Coneflower

* The inventory can hold an unlimited amount of **coneflower** seeds.
* Growing coneflowers is a great way to make energy mix and energy mix+. The inventory can carry 5 of each type. This will cost 10 **coneflower** to make a max of both.
* **Coneflower** is quite common in the game, it is probably the least beneficial plant to place in a garden though it still has large benefits.
* **Coneflower** seeds can be planted in the Wall planter along with Aloe seeds.
* **Coneflower** grows rather quickly

## Trivia

* The coneflower is based on the real life flower, from the *Echinacea* species.

## Bugs

* In multiplayer, there is a chance that the same herb plant will appear differently for different players. For example, what appears as marigold for one player may appear to be coneflower or aloe for another.
