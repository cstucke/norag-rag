The **"She's not dead / She is sleeping"** is a story item that was added in update v0.51 of ***The Forest***.

## Gameplay

**"She's not dead / She is sleeping"** note is written on a Bible page showing Romans 10 and 11. It can be found deep within Cave 7, next to a shrine dedicated to the Red Man. Upon encounter, the player will examine the item before adding it to the notes section of the survival guide.

It is very similar to the "Don't be afraid / Just believe" and "A jealous God punishes a parents fault on their children" notes, which was also probably written by Dr. Cross.

## Theory

It is assumed that the note was authored by Dr. Cross, as it appears to reference his daughter, Megan. Megan is, in fact, dead, and not just sleeping, prior to the events of the story. However, as Dr. Cross knows of the existence of the resurrection obelisk, he most likely sees her death as a very temporary thing, hence him referring to her interim status as "sleeping". Regardless, this note implies that he is not completely mentally stable following her death.
