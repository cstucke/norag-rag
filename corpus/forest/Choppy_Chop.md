# Choppy Chop
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Choppy_Chop
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74715

## Description

*Chop up 50 bodies*

## Guide

Currently obtainable in Single player & Multiplayer.

While the description says to chop up 50 full bodies, the achievement completes after chopping off 50 limbs. This can be achieved by killing 10 cannibals, approximately 2 groups, and chopping off all their limbs, including head. This will also result in lowering Sanity by 55 points.

### Requirements

Chop off 50 limbs
