The **"A jealous God punishes a parents fault on their children"** is a story item that was added in update v1.0 of ***The Forest***

## Gameplay

The note is written on a Bible page. It can be found in Cave 2, near the crushed cave explorer with the compass and the map. It should be noted it is the same Bible page as Don't be afraid / Just believe.

## Theory

* It can be assumed that this note was penned by Dr. Mathew Cross as it was written with a red paint like She's not dead / She is sleeping and Don't be afraid / Just believe bible pages.
* In the Old Testament it is stated more than once that children of sinners will be punished because of their parents sins. An ancient form of the decalogue contains this threat:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “ | *I, Yahweh your God, am a jealous God. I punish a parent’s fault on the children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren of those who hate me.* | „ |
|  | ~ **Exodus 20,5** |  |
