The **Compass** is a navigational tool that was added in update v0.19.

## Gameplay

The compass cannot be equipped with a lighter or flashlight. However, the letters on the compass glow in the dark, removing the need for a light source to illuminate it. The **compass** can also be used to aim down while holding a spear, making fishing easier.

## Location

The **compass** can found in Cave 2 - Hanging Cave on the body of a dead rock climber, along with the Map.
