**Megan Drawings** are a parallel of the Timmy Drawings that the character can find. They are, as the name suggests, created by Megan Cross, daughter of Dr. Mathew Cross.

## Gameplay

Her drawings depict mostly happy, childish things, such as unicorns, while Timmy's drawings often show the environment and cannibals. One Megan drawing suggests that Megan viewed her father as a monster, possibly giving her the motive to kill him. These drawings can be found in the Sahara Research Laboratory and in the Yacht .
