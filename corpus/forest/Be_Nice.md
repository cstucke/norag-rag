# Be Nice
Source: https://theforest.fandom.com/wiki/Be_Nice
Categories: Achievement
Revision: 74697

## Description

*Share a food or drink item in MP*

## Guide

Obtainable only in Multiplayer.

Combine your metal tin tray with any food or drink item on the crafting mat and equip it, then have another player collect the item from the tray.

### Requirements

Share a food or drink item with other player using Metal Tin Tray
