**Liberty Cap Mushrooms** are edible fungi found throughout the Peninsula.

## Gameplay

They can be eaten directly to restore a small amount of hunger and energy, or collected with the pouch. Like berries, collected mushrooms do not spoil and can be eaten later, used for crafting, or planted in a garden. The player can only grow mushrooms in caves; however, they do not spawn there naturally and must be brought in from outside. As of v0.66, they cannot be grown in wall planters.

Using pouch, you can collect up to 10 **Liberty Cap Mushrooms**.

## Trivia

* In reality, the Liberty Cap (*Psilocybe semilanceata*), one of the safely edible mushrooms, is famous for its use as a psychedelic hallucinogen. It is one of the so-called "magic mushrooms".
* The Liberty Cap is usually found in grass that has a lot of nutrients for the mushroom, such as those grazed by sheep.
* Liberty caps only grow in late autumn when the weather is under 10\*C
* The amount of psilocybin (the psycho-active substance) lies between 0.80-1% depending on growing conditions.
* In reality, it would take around 20 mushrooms for a hefty dose.
